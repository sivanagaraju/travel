# Maravanthe Beach

**Circuit role:** Must-cover **NH66 photo stop** (sea on one side, river on the other).  
**When:** **A/B/C/F Sat 14 ~13:40–14:00** going **north**. **D Sun 15** same northbound. **E Mon 16** going **south** after Murudeshwar.

## Pleasant

A **pleasant** Maravanthe is **15–20 minutes**: pull off, one photograph, 6-year-old stretches legs, coconut if a stall is calm, **back in the car**. The highway is the attraction. It is **not** a playground beach.

## Peace

There is visual **peace** (open sea, Souparnika river) and **zero** peace if you try to swim. Currents. Udupi Tourism lists it among beaches with marked safe zones **for looking**, not for a toddler free-for-all.

## Handle kids

- **Hold hands on the shoulder.** NH66 is live.
- **Not a swim.** Look-don’t-swim list.
- Toilet: assume none you trust. Use Murudeshwar or the previous hotel.
- Nap in the carrier or on a lap can start as you leave.

## What this place covers

Highway viewpoint · photo · 15-minute stretch. **Does not** cover a beach picnic, water sports, or a meal stop unless someone has a desperate adult coffee.

## Skip

Treating Maravanthe as “the beach hour.” That hour belongs to Malpe or Kudle.

## Calm vs crowded

A 15-min weekday-style stop **2**. Festival/holiday parking **4**.
