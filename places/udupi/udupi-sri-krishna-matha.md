# Udupi Sri Krishna Matha

**Circuit role:** Must-cover. Car Street, Udupi.  
**When:** **A:** Fri evening after 17:30 **or** Sat 11:00. **C2:** Friday 13 Nov, unhurried. **D:** Sat morning.

## Pleasant

A **pleasant** darshan is **20–40 minutes** through the **Kanakana / Navagraha Kindi** (nine-holed window). Official-style hours **05:00–13:00 and 15:30–21:00** ([shrikrishnamatha.org](https://shrikrishnamatha.org/)). Closed ~13:00–15:30. Dress modestly; men may need the inner-court upper-garment rule in force since Jan 2026 — read the board.

## Peace

**Peace** is **after 15:30 on a weekday** or **before 09:00**. Saturday 11:00 is **3–4**. Anna dasoha exists; with an infant, **Mitra Samaj breakfast** next door is easier than the math queue for food.

## Handle kids

- Stroller in the **outer** street; carrier inside.
- 6-year-old: one story (Kanakadasa, the window) so the wait has a point.
- Mitra Samaj is **breakfast hours ~06:10–12:30**, not dinner. Woodlands / hotel for other meals. High chair: ask; likely lap.
