# Varanga Lake Jain Temple (Kere Basadi & Neminatha Basadi)

**Circuit role:** Breathtaking **medieval water temple & heritage wonder** in the Udupi-foothills belt (An 850-year-old Jain temple standing serenely in the middle of a natural 14-acre lotus lake, accessible only via a gentle wooden boat ride).  
**When to visit:**  
- **Approach H1 / H2:** **Fri 13 Nov 11:30–13:00 PM** (Scenic inland detour en route from Kukke/Karkala to Udupi).  
- **Approach A / B / C1 / C2 / G1 / G2:** **Fri 13 Nov or Sat 14 Nov morning**.  
**Driving distance:** ~38 km northeast of Udupi town (~45 min drive via Manipal-Alevoor-Hebri road); ~24 km north of Karkala (~30 min drive). Located in Varanga village near Hebri.

## 850-Year-Old Lake Sanctuary Lore

Built in the 12th century under the patronage of the Alupa rulers and Jain ascetics, the **Kere Basadi** of Varanga is an architectural marvel of Karnataka.

The sanctum is constructed on a raised granite foundation right in the center of a spring-fed natural lake. The temple features a **Chaturmukha (four-faced)** symmetrical sanctum housing four Tirthankaras carved from lustrous black granite, along with an ancient shrine of Goddess Padmavathi. 

Devotees believe that a flower offering (*Hoo Prasad*) from Goddess Padmavathi brings divine guidance and blessings for family wellbeing and children.

The shore is anchored by the grand **Neminatha Basadi**, featuring a colossal monolithic pillar (*Manasthambha*) and ancient stone inscriptions recording royal grants from medieval kings.

- **Boat Ride Safety for Infants:** The lake is completely sheltered freshwater with zero waves, swell, or current. The boatman uses an electric motor or oars to glide smoothly across the 200-meter span in under 3 minutes. Hold the baby securely in a chest carrier.
- **Kid Highlight (6-year-old):** Buy a packet of puffed rice (*Pori*) at the shore stall for ₹10. As the boat glides across, dozens of harmless freshwater turtles and fish surface to eat from the water—a magical highlight that kids remember for years.
- **Stroller Alert:** Leave the stroller safely in the Innova Crysta. The boat and the stone steps of the island temple require wearing the baby in a carrier.
- **Footwear:** Devotees remove shoes before boarding the boat at the shore jetty. Carry baby socks to protect infant feet on the sun-warmed stone island.
