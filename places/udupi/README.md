# Udupi Coastal, Heritage & Beach Circuit

Udupi is the central anchor of the coastal Karnataka route, combining the 13th-century Madhwa Vaishnavite heritage of Sri Krishna Matha with premier family white-sand beaches, secluded coconut shores, medieval lake temples, and coastal highway vistas.
