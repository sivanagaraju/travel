# Kemmannu Hanging Bridge & Backwaters, Udupi

**Circuit role:** Premier **peaceful river island & backwater gem** in the Udupi region (A rustic wooden cable suspension bridge swinging over the serene Swarna/Suvarna river backwaters, connecting tiny coconut island hamlets, with gentle country boat rides and mangrove kayaking).  
**When to visit:**  
- **Day 2 (Fri 13 Nov 16:30–18:30 PM):** Combined with Delta Beach (Kodi Bengre) as the premier calm backwater alternative to Malpe or Kapu.  
**Location:** Tonse East, Kemmannu (~8 km north of Udupi town center, ~15 min drive; ~7 km east of Delta Beach).

## Why This Hidden Gem is Exceptional

1. **Gentle, Wave-Free Water:** While the Arabian Sea has surf, undertow, and salt spray, the Swarna river backwaters at Kemmannu are calm like a mirror. A 6-year-old child and a 1-year-old baby can experience peaceful water views without any wave fright.
2. **The Suspension Bridge Adventure:** Walking across the gently swaying wooden bridge over emerald green water surrounded by coconut groves is an unforgettable highlight for children.
3. **Pristine Mangrove Sanctuary:** Lush green mangroves shelter kingfishers, egrets, and cormorants along the riverbanks.


- **Baby Gear:** **Strollers cannot cross the wooden suspension bridge**. Keep the infant in an ergonomic front chest carrier.
- **Safety:** The bridge is bordered by protective cable netting, but adults must hold the 6-year-old child's hand while walking across.
- **Country Boat Rides:** Local boatmen operate slow, stable wooden rowing canoes through the mangrove channels (life jackets provided; baby held securely on parent's lap).
