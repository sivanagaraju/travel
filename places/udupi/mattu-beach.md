# Mattu Beach (The Peaceful Sunset & Coconut Shore)

**Circuit role:** Highly recommended **tranquil, crowd-free alternative beach** in the Udupi region (A secluded golden sand beach fringed by endless coconut groves, renowned for pristine sunsets, calm shallow surf, and the famous **Mattu Gulla** GI-tag brinjal heritage).  
**When to visit:**  
- **Approach H2 (2-Base Sanctuary):** **Fri 13 Nov 16:30–18:30 PM** (The premier peaceful sunset alternative to bustling Malpe Beach).  
- **Approach A / B / C1 / C2 / G1 / G2:** **Fri 13 Nov or Sat 14 Nov afternoon/sunset**.  
**Driving distance:** ~10 km south of Malpe Beach (~18 min drive via coastal road); ~10 km southwest of Udupi town (~15 min drive via Katapadi junction).

## The Mattu Gulla Lore & Bioluminescent Magic

Unlike commercialized Malpe Beach with its water sports, noisy throngs, and speedboats, **Mattu Beach** is an authentic, unspoiled coastal haven. 

The village of Mattu is celebrated for **Mattu Gulla**, a rare variety of light-green brinjal with a designated Geographical Indication (GI) tag. According to 500-year-old lore, the great Madhwa saint Sri Vadiraja Tirtha of Sodhe Matha gifted the sacred seeds to the local farmers. To this day, the first harvest of Mattu Gulla is reverently offered to Lord Krishna at the Udupi Sri Krishna Matha.

In winter months, Mattu Beach is also famous along the coastal circuit as a prime viewing ground for natural **noctiluca bioluminescence** (sea sparkle), where nighttime ocean waves occasionally glow with ethereal blue light under dark, moonless skies.

## Pleasant Window

- **Sunset Window (16:30–18:30 PM on Friday 13 Nov):**
  - Temperatures drop into the comfortable 26°C range with cool onshore sea breezes.
  - The sun sets directly over the Arabian Sea unobstructed by commercial shacks or crowds.
  - The narrow coastal road allows parents to drive directly alongside the sand, park safely under palm trees, and step out onto the beach without walking across long scorching parking lots.

- **Stroller Feasibility:** The narrow coastal tarmac road running between the coconut groves and the beach provides a completely level, paved walking path for a stroller.
- **Safe Water Play:** The beach has a very gentle slope with no sudden drop-offs. The 1-year-old infant can sit safely on the wet sand at the water's edge while gentle wave foam washes over their feet.
- **Restroom & Facilities:** Being an undeveloped village beach, there are no commercial restrooms on the sand. Use the clean facilities at your Malpe resort (18 min away) or at Katapadi junction (7 min away).
