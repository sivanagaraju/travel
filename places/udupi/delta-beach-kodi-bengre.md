# Delta Beach (Kodi Bengre Estuary), Udupi

**Circuit role:** Optional **peaceful offbeat sunset & estuary peninsula** (where the Suvarna River merges into the Arabian Sea).  
**When to visit:** Approach C2 (Udupi 2-night base): **Fri 13 Nov 16:30–18:00**.  
**Location:** Kodi Bengre peninsula, ~9 km north of Malpe Beach (~15 min scenic drive).

## Pleasant Window
- **16:30–18:00 (Sunset Magic):** The drive along the narrow Kodi Bengre spit is lined with tall coconut palms on both sides, with the sea visible on your left and the river on your right.
- At the tip (Delta Point), the water is serene and calm. Toddlers can safely play on the quiet river-side sand spits without being buffeted by ocean breakers.

---

## Peace & Atmosphere
- Delta Beach is a tranquil antidote to the carnival crowds of main Malpe.
- There are **no water sports, no loud shacks, and no commercial stalls**. Local fishermen mend nets under palm trees while migratory birds wade in the shallow river shoals.
