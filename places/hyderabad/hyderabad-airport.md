# Hyderabad Airport (HYD)

**Circuit role:** Start and end. Not a tourist stop.  
**Approaches:** All (A–F). Outbound morning to **IXE** in A/B/C/D/F; to **HBX** in E. Return from the other airport.

## Pleasant

A **pleasant** departure is a **05:00-hour** check-in with the infant already fed, not a 03:00 scramble. Book the **bulkhead** if IndiGo will give it. Pack idli/banana in a cabin pouch so you are not buying airport chilli at 04:30. Return evening (Fly91 ~22:55 into HYD on A/C/D/F) is pleasant only if you did **not** do a 05:00 Gokarna start.

## Peace

There is no scenic **peace** at HYD. The peace you can manufacture: one adult walks the 6-year-old while the other does security with the sleeping infant; noise-cancelling for the 6-year-old on the ATR; no last-minute SIM/cash errands in the terminal.

## Handle kids

- Infant **lap ticket** + extra cabin bag for diapers and a full change.
- Stroller: gate-check. **Carrier:** wear through security and in the Innova / Hyderabad cab (on a lap). **No car seat.**
- Formula/milk you already use. Airport cafes are not a first-food experiment.

## What this place covers

Check-in, security, ATR flight to Mangalore or Hubli, reverse on the way back. Toilets airside. No temple, no beach.

## Skip

Any plan to “drive HYD to the coast instead.” ~16 hours with a one-year-old is how the trip fails before Kukke.

## Calm vs crowded

**3–4** at 05:00 domestic. Worse on Monday mornings. Still easier than a ghat.
