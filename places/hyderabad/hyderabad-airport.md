## Kids / Baby Logistics & Flying with an Infant

1. **The "Sleep-Through" Strategy:**
   - Do not wake the 1-year-old infant to bathe them in the dead of night. Dress them the night before in the soft cotton onesie they will fly in.
   - At 04:35 AM, gently transfer the sleeping baby directly into the baby carrier or car seat without turning on harsh bedroom lights.
   - The baby will usually sleep right through the 35-minute cab ride on the Outer Ring Road.
2. **Airport Security Liquid Exemption:**
   - Aviation security regulations across Indian airports explicitly **permit baby milk, prepared formula bottles, and baby drinking water exceeding 100 ml** when accompanied by an infant.
   - Inform the CISF security officer at the X-ray conveyor: *"Baby food and boiled water for 1-year-old"*. They will scan the flask separately and return it immediately.
3. **ATR-72 Turboprop Ear Pressure Protection:**
   - Flight 6E 7581 is operated on an ATR-72 / A320 aircraft. Cabin pressurization is noticeable during rapid descents.
   - **Crucial Action:** Begin nursing the baby or offering a bottle of warm milk or pacifier **the moment the aircraft engines spool up for takeoff (06:50 AM)**, and repeat **during the descent into Mangalore** (around 08:15 AM). Swallowing continuously opens the Eustachian tubes and prevents painful ear popping.

## Return Landing at HYD (Fly91 Inbound ~22:55 PM)

- When landing back at Hyderabad from Hubli around 22:55 PM, your stroller will be handed back to you at the aircraft door / baggage carousel.
- Prepaid airport taxis (Uber, Ola, Meru, or Mega Cabs) are available 24/7 on Level 1 (Ground Transportation).
- Within 40 minutes via the ORR, the family will be home in Hyderabad, having completed an unforgettable 1st birthday holiday!
