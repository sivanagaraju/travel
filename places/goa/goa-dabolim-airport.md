# South Goa Dabolim Airport (GOI) & Coastal Transit Corridor

**Circuit role:** **PREMIER RECOMMENDED RETURN EXIT AIRPORT** for the Coastal Karnataka family tour back to Hyderabad (Alternative to Hubli HBX).  
**Driving route & distance:** Gokarna to Goa Dabolim Airport (GOI) is **~140–145 km (~3 to 3.5 hours driving)** via NH66 North through Karwar, Pollem border, Canacona, and Margao bypass.  
**Critical flight options to Hyderabad (HYD):**  
- **IndiGo 6E 6116 (19:40 PM Departure → 21:00 PM HYD Arrival, 1h 20m Airbus A320):** **The Gold Standard for families.** Allows relaxed 11:00 AM checkout from Gokarna, scenic coastal lunch in Karwar, arrival at GOI by 16:30–17:00, and home in Hyderabad before 22:30 PM.  
- **Air India Express IX 984 (21:30 PM Departure → 22:55 PM HYD Arrival, 1h 25m Boeing 737-800):** **Late Evening Alternative.** Generous time buffer for an afternoon beach walk or dinner in South Goa.  
- **IndiGo 6E 6032 (14:15 PM Departure → 15:40 PM HYD Arrival, 1h 25m Airbus A320):** **Afternoon Option.** Early 08:30 AM departure from Gokarna reaches Hyderabad before tea time.

## Crowd & Terminal Experience

- **Crowd Rating:** **2 to 3 / 5** during afternoon and early evening hours. Following the opening of Mopa Airport (GOX) in North Goa, Dabolim (GOI) has become noticeably quieter, calmer, and much faster for domestic travelers.
- **Queue Times:** Security queues rarely exceed 10–15 minutes.
- **Family Cleanliness:** Restrooms and child-care facilities are maintained to high AAI international standards.
- **Baby Care:** Dedicated mother & child room located airside near Gate 3 with comfortable nursing chairs, changing tables, and baby food heating facilities.
