# Hubli Airport (HBX)

**Circuit role:** Must-cover **exit** on A/B/C/D/F. **Entry** on E.  
**Drive:** Gokarna → HBX **147–168 km, 3–4 h. FLAG** vs 2.5 h / ~180 km. Do not shrink the number.

## Pleasant

A **pleasant** HBX day is an **afternoon** leave from Gokarna (**13:00**) for an **evening** flight (Fly91 **~21:25**). Two feeding stops. Small airport, stroller-easy. A **09:55** IndiGo means a **05:00** Gokarna start — not pleasant with a one-year-old the day after a beach binge.

## Peace

No scenery mandate. **Peace** is a quiet Innova, snacks you packed, and not arguing with the driver about “one more temple.” Yellapur-ish toilet stop at 14:30 and 16:00. Arrive HBX **17:00–17:30**, never after 18:00 on a ghat-ish inland road.

## Handle kids

- Infant in the **carrier** or on a lap until the terminal door.
- Break every **2 hours**, written on the voucher.
- Evening flight: milk, diapers, change in the small HBX toilet before boarding.
- If Approach **E** (HBX inbound): only if a **morning** HYD→HBX exists so you reach Kudle before 18:00.

## What this place covers

Domestic terminal · HYD flights (IndiGo ~1 h 20 m, Fly91 ~1 h 30 m) · no tourist circuit inside Hubli city (Unkal Lake etc. are **not** on this trip).

## Skip

Hubli sightseeing, 09:55 flight unless it is the last metal, driving past 18:00 to “make” a late check-in.

## Calm vs crowded

Airport **2**. Road into Hubli late afternoon **2–3**.
