# Hubli Airport (HBX) & Inland Transit Corridor

**Circuit role:** Must-cover **exit airport** for Approaches A, B, C, D, and F (Entry airport for reverse Approach E).  
**Driving route & distance:** Gokarna to Hubli Airport (HBX) is **~155 km (~3.5 to 4 hours driving)** via NH66 North, NH63 Arbail Ghat, and Yellapur.  
**Critical flight timing:**  
- **Fly91 IC 3402 (21:25 PM Departure → 22:55 PM HYD Arrival, 1h 30m ATR-72-600):** **The Gold Standard for this family.** Allows a civilized 13:00 PM checkout from Gokarna, two feeding breaks, and arrival at HBX by 17:30 PM.  
- **IndiGo 6E 7416 (09:55 AM Departure → 11:15 AM HYD Arrival, 1h 20m ATR-72):** **STRICT SKIP / AVOID.** Requires waking a 1-year-old infant at 04:30 AM and navigating a foggy ghat in darkness.

## Pleasant Window
- **Late Afternoon Transit (13:00–17:30 PM):** Departing Gokarna at 13:00 allows driving the Arbail Ghat entirely in bright daylight, catching a leisurely feeding stop in Yellapur, and arriving at HBX airport relaxed at 17:30.

---

## Kids / Baby Logistics & Ghat Drive Survival

1. **Why 13:00 PM Departure is Essential:**
   - Departing at 13:00 PM synchronizes perfectly with the infant's afternoon nap schedule. The rhythmic hum of the Innova Crysta on NH66 puts the baby to sleep for the first 1.5 hours through Ankola and the initial Arbail Ghat curves.
2. **Combating Motion Sickness in Arbail Ghat:**
   - The climb through Arbail Ghat has continuous gentle curves.
   - Keep the car cabin well-ventilated and cool.
   - Avoid giving heavy greasy foods to the 6-year-old child immediately before entering the ghat; light crackers, bananas, and sips of water are optimal.
3. **The Yellapur Sanctuary (Banana County):**
   - Do not stop at random roadside dhabas with filthy squat toilets.
   - Direct your driver to pull into **Banana County Resort** (~2 km off the highway near Yellapur) or **Highway Comfort**. These properties offer hygienic restrooms, shaded garden lawns where the 6-year-old can run, and clean kitchens willing to warm milk for the baby.

## Airport Features & Flying Out on Fly91

- **Hubli Airport (HBX) Advantage:**
  - Unlike massive metro airports (such as HYD or BLR) where walking from security to the boarding gate takes 25 minutes, HBX is a compact, modern regional airport. Walking distance from the curb to the aircraft door is less than 120 meters.
  - Check-in queues rarely exceed 5–8 passengers.
- **Dharwad Pedha Souvenir:**
  - Before entering the terminal, stop at the *Big Mishra Pedha* outlet near the Hubli airport junction to buy authentic GI-tagged *Dharwad Pedha* and *Belgaum Kunda* for family back home in Hyderabad.
