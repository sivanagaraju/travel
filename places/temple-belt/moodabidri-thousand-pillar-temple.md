# Moodabidri Thousand Pillar Temple (Saavira Kambada Basadi)

**Circuit role:** Optional **serene heritage halt** directly on the route between Dharmasthala/Karkala and Udupi (known as the "Jain Varanasi of the South").  
**When to visit:** Approach A/C/D: 30–45 min stop when driving past Moodabidri (15:00–16:00).  
**Location:** Moodabidri town, ~16 km south of Karkala, right along NH169.

## Pleasant Window
- **15:00–16:30:** The Basadi compound is shielded from highway traffic. The covered granite halls remain remarkably cool even on hot afternoons.
- Operating hours: **09:00 AM – 01:00 PM** and **02:00 PM – 06:00 PM**. Entry is **Free** (nominal ₹10–20 donation box).

---

## Peace & Atmosphere
- Built in 1430 AD by the local ruler Devaraya Vodeya, this 3-storey granite monument is renowned because among its **1,000 carved pillars, no two carvings are identical**.
- Unlike crowded pilgrimage sites, Moodabidri is an oasis of calm. Monks and caretakers maintain an unhurried, welcoming atmosphere.
- **Top Feature for Families:** **Zero hill climbing.** Unlike Karkala's stone steps, Moodabidri Basadi is located on completely level ground, making it effortlessly accessible with a stroller or for tired parents.

## What This Place Covers
- 1,000 carved granite pillars spanning 7 pavilions (mandapas).
- 50-foot monolithic pillar (Manastambha) in the courtyard.
- Bronze idol of 8th Tirthankara Lord Chandranatha.
