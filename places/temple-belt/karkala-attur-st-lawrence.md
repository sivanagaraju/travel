## Heritage Lore: The Miracle of the Gridiron

Dating back to 1759, the shrine was founded when local Christians, fleeing religious persecutions under Tipu Sultan, carried an ancient wooden statue of St. Lawrence in search of safety. Resting at the spring of Attur, they placed the statue down. When they attempted to lift it, the statue became miraculously immobile. Recognizing divine will, they built a small shrine here.

Today, Pope Francis elevated the shrine to the status of **Minor Basilica** in 2016. What makes Attur extraordinary is its universal harmony: more than half of the daily visitors are Hindu and Muslim families seeking blessings for their children, health, and peace of mind. The sacred natural spring feeds the **Pushkarini (Holy Pond)**, creating a tranquil sanctuary.
