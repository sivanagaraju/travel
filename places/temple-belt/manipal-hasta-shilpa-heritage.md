## The Vision of Hasta Shilpa: Preserving Vanishing Canara

Conceived by the visionary heritage conservationist **Vijayanath Shenoy**, Hasta Shilpa is not a static museum—it is a reconstructed historic village.

When centuries-old ancestral homes (*Guthu Mane*) across Coastal Karnataka and the Deccan plateau were being demolished for modern concrete buildings, Shenoy painstakingly dismantled them piece by piece—every carved wooden pillar, terracotta roof tile, and stone lintel—and meticulously rebuilt them on this 7-acre campus in Manipal.

Walking through the village feels like traveling back 300 years into the tranquil coastal villages of medieval Karnataka. The shaded forest canopy keeps the temperature several degrees cooler than the open road, making it a peaceful cultural interlude for families.

- **Stroller vs Carrier:** Paved outdoor pathways accommodate strollers smoothly. However, traditional coastal homes feature raised wooden thresholds (*Hostilu*); wearing the baby in a chest carrier allows parents to step inside the historic rooms effortlessly.
- **Kid Engagement (6-year-old):** The 6yo child will be fascinated by the historic wooden swings (*Toogu Uyyale*), carved animal pillars (wooden horses and elephants), and vintage toy exhibits.
- **Facilities:** Clean air-conditioned reception lobby with spotless Western restrooms, cold drinking water, and baby diaper-changing space.
