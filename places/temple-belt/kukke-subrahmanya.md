# Kukke Subrahmanya Temple

**Circuit role:** Must-cover **ghat temple**. Weekday only.  
**When:** **A/B/D/F Fri 13 Nov morning**. **C1 Thu 12 overnight**. **C2 Thu 12 day-stop**. **E Tue 17 afternoon** (harder).

## Pleasant

A **pleasant** Kukke is **not a weekend** and **not Champa Shashti**. For 2026 that festival’s main day is **15 Dec**, mahotsavam ~1–15 Dec — you are safe on dates, still keep **Fri/Thu**. Arrive 09:30–11:30, darshan, annadana, leave. Do **not** add Kumaradhara bath with an infant.

## Peace

**Peace** is the river-and-hill setting once you are out of the inner queue. Guest-house dusk in **C1** is the only way to feel Kukke without a stopwatch. On A, you will not get that — you get a clean weekday darshan and you go.

## Handle kids

- **Carrier**, not stroller, in the sanctum path.
- Stone floors, serpents as motif — tell the 6-year-old before you enter so it is wonder, not fear.
- Special/VIP counter if the infant melts (confirm ₹ on [itms.kar.nic.in](https://itms.kar.nic.in)).
- Annadana ~11:30–14:00: rice, sambar, curd. Lap + mat. No high chair.
- Ghat **after Uppinangady**. **No driving this road after 18:00.** Nap in the carrier or on a lap on the way in.

## Skip

Weekend visit, festival fortnight, pushing darshan past 13:30 if you still need Dharmasthala’s 14:20 slot (Approach A).

## Calm vs crowded

Weekday morning **3**. Weekend **4–5**. 15 Dec **5**.
