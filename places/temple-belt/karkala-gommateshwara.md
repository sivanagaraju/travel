# Karkala Gommateshwara Monolith (Bahubali Statue)

**Circuit role:** Optional **scenic viewpoint & giant monolith stop** in Karkala (pair with Chaturmukha Basadi).  
**When to visit:** Approach A/C: 16:45–17:15 (short 20-min visit right after Chaturmukha Basadi).  
**Location:** Gommatagiri Hill, Karkala (~1 km from Chaturmukha Basadi).

## Pleasant Window
- **16:45–17:15 (Sunset Glow):** Catching the 42-foot single-stone colossus of Lord Bahubali as the setting sun illuminates the grey granite statue with golden light.
- **Drive-Up Secret for Families:** Many tourists climb from the base (~100 steep steps). **Tell your driver to take the rear motorable road to the hilltop parking!** This leaves only ~25 gentle steps to reach the statue platform.

---

## Peace & Atmosphere
- Erected in 1432 AD by Veera Pandya Bhairarasa, this is the second tallest monolith in Karnataka (after Shravanabelagola).
- Set atop an open granite mound with constant mountain breezes and panoramic views of Anekere lake.
