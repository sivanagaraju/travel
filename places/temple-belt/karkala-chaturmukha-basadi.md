# Chaturmukha Basadi, Karkala

**Circuit role:** Must-cover **quiet** Jain temple on the Dharmasthala→Udupi road.  
**When:** **A/B/C2/F ~16:00–16:40** (second window). **C1/D 10:00–14:00** (better light, less rush).

## Pleasant

A **pleasant** stop is **20–30 minutes**: granite four-face temple, 108 pillars, view toward the Bahubali hill. Hours **10:00–14:00 and 16:00–17:30**, **free**. [Karnataka Tourism](https://karnatakatourism.org/tour-item/chaturmukha-basadi/).

## Peace

This is the **peace** stop of the temple belt. Usually **no queue**. Speak softly; it is a living Basadi. Photography often banned **inside**.

## Handle kids

- **~80–150 stone steps.** Stroller **at the car**. Infant in **carrier**. 6-year-old can climb if not depleted — one adult behind.
- 20 minutes is enough. Do not add the Bahubali statue climb the same afternoon on Approach A.
- Water in the car. Little shade on the rock.

## What this place covers

Chaturmukha Basadi (must) · optional glance at Gommateshwara on the opposite hill **without** the full stair if time is gone · Karkala town is not a food stop unless you are on C1/D with a clock.

## Skip

Varanga Kere Basadi (27 km extra). Forcing the 16:00 slot if you are still in Dharmasthala at 15:30 — go to Udupi and skip rather than drive angry.

## Calm vs crowded

**1.** Festival Jain days rare for your dates. Highest this trip gets to is **2**.
