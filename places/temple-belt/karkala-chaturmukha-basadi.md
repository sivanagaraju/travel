# Chaturmukha Basadi, Karkala

**Circuit role:** Must-cover **Jain architectural masterpiece & serene hilltop halt** (on the Dharmasthala → Udupi road).  
**When to visit:** Approach A/B/C2/F: **Fri 13 Nov 16:00–16:40** (second opening window). Approach C1/D: **10:00–14:00**.  
**Driving distance:** ~64 km from Dharmasthala (~1 h 30 min); ~38 km to Udupi (~45 min).

## Pleasant Window
- **16:00–16:40 (Golden Hour Breeze):** The afternoon sun softens by 16:00. The hilltop stone cools down, and an invigorating cross-breeze sweeps through the open colonnaded halls.
- Operating Hours: **10:00 AM – 02:00 PM** and **04:00 PM – 05:30 PM**. Entry is **Free**.

---

## Peace & Spiritual Vibe
- This is universally agreed by travelers and bloggers to be the **most tranquil stop in the entire inland temple belt**.
- Completed in 1586 AD by Immadi Bhairarasa Odeya, the temple features 4 identical carved entrances facing the 4 cardinal directions, supported by 108 beautifully proportioned pillars.
- There are **no aggressive priests, no ticketing lines, and no commercial hawkers**. Devotees and visitors speak in hushed tones, surrounded only by the rustling wind and panoramic mountain views.

- **Rock Steps:** The climb takes ~5–7 minutes. The steps are wide and low, but there are no handrails; keep firm hold of the 6-year-old child's hand.
- **Photography:** Photography of the exterior and courtyard is allowed and spectacular; photography of the inner sanctum tirthankaras is respectfully prohibited.
- **Footwear:** Shoes must be removed at the base of the temple platform. Stone is comfortable by 16:00.

---

## What This Place Covers
- Symmetrical four-faced granite basadi with 108 pillars.
- Black polished granite statues of Lord Adinatha, Shantinatha, and Munisuvrata.
- 360-degree vantage point overlooking Karkala town and Gommateshwara rock.

---

## What to Skip
- **Varanga Kere Basadi (Lake Temple):** Situated 26 km north of Karkala. Requires waiting for an irregular row-boat ferry. Skip on Friday so you don't compromise arrival in Udupi before dusk.
