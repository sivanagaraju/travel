## The Floating Chariot Lore & Campco Culture

Built during the reign of the Alupa dynasty in the 12th century, the temple is steeped in coastal legend. 

The temple tank, **Bangarada Kere (Golden Pond)**, is famous for the historical miracle where a massive temple chariot fell into the water centuries ago and miraculously floated across the sacred tank during festival ceremonies. Devotees revere Lord Mahalingeshwara as the protector of town prosperity and health.

Just 500 meters from the temple stands the head office and central processing unit of **CAMPCO (The Central Arecanut and Cocoa Marketing and Processing Co-operative Limited)**, Asia's largest cooperative chocolate manufacturer. Visiting the retail outlet allows the 6yo child to pick up fresh chocolates, malt drinks, and chocolate wafers straight from the factory store.
