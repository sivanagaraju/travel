# Dharmasthala (Manjunatha Temple)

**Circuit role:** Must-cover.  
**When:** **A/B/C2/F Fri or Thu 13:30–14:20** (hit the gate before the ~14:30 close). **C1/D morning 06:30–11:00** plus Annadana if you slept nearby.

## Pleasant

A **pleasant** Dharmasthala is **Annadana + a short darshan**, not a museum-and-Bahubali-climb day. Standard windows: **06:30–11:00, 12:15–14:30, 17:00–20:30** (confirm locally). Sannidhana ~₹200 if the general line is eating the infant.

## Peace

The **peace** is the dining hall’s rhythm — thousands fed, no bill, veg, fast — and the courtyard if you skip Ratnagiri steps. Inner sanctum is **not** quiet. Do not wait through the 14:30–17:00 close with a toddler in a car park.

## Handle kids

- Stroller to the outer complex; **carrier** in queue.
- Annadana: 11:00–14:30/14:45 typical (longer Sun/Mon). Curd rice is the infant’s friend.
- No high chairs. Mat in the bag.
- If you overnight (D or C1): temple guest house via official booking — hot water usually yes; crib **no**.

## Skip

Full-day “Dharmasthala plus Kukke plus Udupi” without the FLAG in writing. That is how 18:00 ghat happens.

## Calm vs crowded

Always **3–4**. Morning 06:30 **3**. Midday **4**.
