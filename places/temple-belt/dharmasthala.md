# Dharmasthala (Sri Manjunatha Swamy Temple)

**Circuit role:** Must-cover **historic pilgrimage center** on the Western Ghat foothills (Lord Shiva worshipped under Jain administration).  
**When to visit:** Approach G2: **Thu 12 Nov check-in & evening darshan under illuminations**. Approach G1 / C1 / D: **Fri 13 Nov morning (09:45–13:00) with Annadana lunch**. Approach A/B/C2/F: **Fri 13 Nov 13:15–14:20** (MUST enter sanctum queue before the 14:30 afternoon close).  
**Driving distance:** ~53 km from Kukke Subrahmanya (~1 h 15 min via Dharmasthala-Subramanya Rd); ~64 km to Karkala (~1.5 h).

## Pleasant Window
- **Friday 13:15–14:20 Window (Approach A):** Timing is paramount! The inner sanctum of Lord Manjunatha closes at **14:30** for afternoon poojas until 17:00. You must pass through the security door before 14:20.
- **Alternative Morning Window (Approach C1/D):** If staying overnight nearby, 06:30–09:00 AM offers cool morning breezes, unhurried darshan, and fresh morning prasada.

---

## Peace & Spiritual Harmony
- Dharmasthala is extraordinary in Indian heritage: Lord Shiva (Manjunatha) is the presiding deity, the priests are Madhwa Vaishnavites, and the temple administration is headed by the Jain Heggade family (Dharmadhikari Dr. D. Veerendra Heggade).
- The hallmark of Dharmasthala is **Dharma Daana (charity), Annadaana (feeding), and Abhaya Daana (solace)**.
- Witnessing the clockwork harmony of the Annapoorna dining hall feeding thousands without a single rupee charged is an unforgettable experience for both adults and the 6-year-old child.

- **Special Darshan Ticket:** Purchase the ₹200 special darshan ticket at the counter to bypass the lengthy general queue. Takes ~15–20 minutes instead of 60–90 minutes.
- **Dress Code:** Men must remove shirts and banyans before entering the inner sanctum. Traditional Indian wear for women (saree or salwar).
- **Baby Hydration:** Dharmasthala gets warm between 12:00 and 15:00. Carry an insulated flask of boiled drinking water and hydration salts for kids.

## What This Place Covers
- Sri Manjunatha Swamy sanctum darshan.
- Free lunch at Annapoorna Annadana dining hall (clean stainless steel tables; high hygiene standards).
- Beautiful temple exterior architecture.

---

## What to Skip on Approach A's Friday
- **Bahubali Monolith on Ratnagiri Hill:** Requires climbing hundreds of exposed stone steps in the afternoon sun. With a 1-year-old in arms and a long drive to Udupi ahead, **STRICT SKIP on Friday**. You will see the Karkala Basadi later in the afternoon anyway.
- **Manjusha Vintage Car Museum:** Fascinating collection, but save time on Friday so you reach Udupi before dusk.
