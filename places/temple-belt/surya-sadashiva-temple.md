**Circuit role:** Extraordinary, highly unique **family pilgrimage shrine** on the **Kukke-to-Dharmasthala corridor** (Famous across India for **Clay Offerings / Matti Harake**, where families pray for and celebrate the gift of children and infants).  
**When to visit:**  
- **Approach G1 / G2 / H1:** **Fri 13 Nov 10:15–11:00 AM** (En route between Southadka and Dharmasthala).  
- **Approach C1 / D:** **Morning 08:30–10:00 AM**.  
**Driving distance:** ~40 km northwest of Kukke Subrahmanya (~55 min); ~14 km southeast of Dharmasthala (~20 min drive via Ujire-Nada road). Located in Nada village off the main highway.

## The Miracle of Matti Harake (Clay Offerings for Children)

Surya Sadashiva Rudra Temple is one of the most distinctive sacred sanctuaries in Karnataka. Devotees visit this shrine seeking the blessing of a child (*Santana Prapti*), health for an ailing infant, or success in life.

When a prayer is answered, the devotee does not offer cash or gold—they return to Surya Temple and offer a **miniature clay replica (*Matti Harake*)** of what they prayed for!
- Couples blessed with a child offer a **miniature terracotta cradle with a clay baby inside**.
- Those whose children overcame an illness offer clay figures of healthy babies.
- Farmers offer clay cows; families moving to a new home offer clay cottages.

Stepping onto the **Harake Bena** terrace and seeing thousands of handcrafted clay cradles basking under the gentle Western Ghats sun is an emotionally moving experience, especially for a family traveling to celebrate an **infant's 1st birthday**.

- **Participating with the Kids:** Let the 6-year-old child pick a terracotta cradle or clay toy from the village potter stall (₹50–₹100). The child can place it at the designated prayer platform as an offering for the baby's long life and health.
- **Dress Code:** Traditional modest Indian wear (dhoti/kurta or formal trousers for men; saree or salwar for women). Men remove shirts in the inner sanctum.
- **Restrooms:** Basic rural restrooms on site; cleaner luxury facilities located at Ujire town (7 km away).
