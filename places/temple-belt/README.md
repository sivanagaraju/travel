# Inland Temple Belt & Foothills Corridor Master Guide

The inland corridor connecting Mangalore, the Western Ghats foothills, and the coastal plains of Udupi and Malpe. This region holds one of India's densest concentrations of historic temples, sacred river confluences, open-air shrines, and medieval architectural wonders.

## Detailed Corridor Breakdowns

### Corridor 1: Mangalore to Kukke Subrahmanya (~105 km, 2.5–3 h)
* **Route:** Mangalore Airport (IXE) $ightarrow$ NH73 via Bantwal bypass $ightarrow$ Mani $ightarrow$ Uppinangady $ightarrow$ Kadaba $ightarrow$ Kukke Subrahmanya.
* **Road Condition:** Excellent 2-lane & 4-lane national highway until Uppinangady; scenic shaded rural ghat road with gentle curves from Uppinangady to Kukke.
* **Places You Can Cover on this Corridor:**
  1. **[Uppinangady Sri Sahasralingeshwara & Sangama](uppinangady-sahasralingeshwara.md)**: Sacred river confluence of Kumaradhara and Netravathi ("Dakshina Kashi"). Perfect midway feeding/rest stop ~55 km from Mangalore. (Terrace river view safe for baby; do not descend water steps).
  2. **[Puttur Sri Mahalingeshwara & CAMPCO Chocolate](puttur-mahalingeshwara.md)**: Ancient 12th-century Shiva temple with sacred Bangarada Kere (Golden Pond) + retail chocolate outlet of Asia's largest cooperative chocolate factory (fun treat for the 6yo!).
  3. **[Kukke Subrahmanya Temple](kukke-subrahmanya.md)**: Presiding deity Lord Subrahmanya under Kumara Parvatha peak. Supreme protector of young children and infants.

---

### Corridor 2: Kukke Subrahmanya to Dharmasthala (~54 km, 1 h 15 min)
* **Route:** Kukke Subrahmanya $ightarrow$ Gundya / Kadaba cross $ightarrow$ Kokkada $ightarrow$ Dharmasthala (via SH114 / Subramanya-Dharmasthala Road).
* **Road Condition:** Scenic rural state highway through areca nut plantations and dense evergreen foothills. Gentle curves, smooth asphalt.
* **Places You Can Cover on this Corridor:**
  1. **[Southadka Sri Mahaganapathi Kshetra](southadka-ganapathi.md)**: Unique open-air shrine inside a sacred bamboo grove with thousands of hanging brass bells. 100% stroller friendly, sweet Avalakki prasadam, shaded cow shelter (*Goshala*).
  2. **[Surya Sadashiva Rudra Temple](surya-sadashiva-temple.md)**: World-famous folk shrine for **Clay Model Offerings (*Matti Harake*)**. Devotees offer miniature terracotta cradles (*Matti Tottilu*) when blessed with children/infants. Breathtaking hillside covered in thousands of terracotta cradles!
  3. **[Dharmasthala Sri Manjunatha Swamy](dharmasthala.md)**: Historic pilgrimage center of Lord Shiva worshipped under Madhwa Vaishnava priests and administered by the Jain Heggade family. World's largest automated free Annapoorna Annadana dining hall.

---

### Corridor 3: Dharmasthala to Malpe / Udupi (~102 km, 2 h 15 min)
* **Route:** Dharmasthala $ightarrow$ Ujire $ightarrow$ Belthangady $ightarrow$ Bajagoli $ightarrow$ Karkala $ightarrow$ Moodbidri / Manipal $ightarrow$ Udupi $ightarrow$ Malpe Beach.
* **Road Condition:** Smooth 2-lane inland highway (SH37) connecting the foothills to the coastal plain.
* **Places You Can Cover on this Corridor:**
  1. **[Karkala Chaturmukha Basadi](karkala-chaturmukha-basadi.md)**: 16th-century Jain architectural masterpiece with 108 polished granite pillars and 4 identical entrances on a breezy granite hill.
  2. **[Karkala Gommateshwara Monolith](karkala-gommateshwara.md)**: 42-foot monolithic statue of Lord Bahubali atop Gommatagiri; car drives right to the top terrace.
  3. **[St. Lawrence Shrine Minor Basilica (Attur, Karkala)](karkala-attur-st-lawrence.md)**: 18th-century miracle shrine with holy Pushkarini pond, miraculous statue, and 100% stroller-friendly manicured European-style gardens.
  4. **[Moodbidri Thousand Pillar Temple (Saavira Kambada Basadi)](moodabidri-thousand-pillar-temple.md)**: Masterwork of Vijayanagara granite carving with 1,000 unique pillars, 100% level stone flooring.
  5. **[Hasta Shilpa Heritage Village (Manipal)](manipal-hasta-shilpa-heritage.md)**: Celebrated open-air living heritage museum featuring 26 authentic restored traditional mansions, courtyards, and timber homes of Coastal Karnataka. Just 12 min from Malpe Beach!
