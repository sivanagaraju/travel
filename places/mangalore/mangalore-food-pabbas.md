## The Non-Negotiable Legend: Pabbas (Ideal Ice Cream)
- **Location:** Lalbagh, MG Road, Mangalore. (Branch of Ideal Ice Cream).
- **Hours:** 09:30 AM – 09:30 PM daily.
- **Iconic Orders:**
  - **Gadbad (₹120–150):** Layered tall glass with strawberry and vanilla scoops, dry fruits, fresh jelly, fruit cuts, and kesar syrup.
  - **Dilkush (₹110):** Rich vanilla and kesar ice cream sandwich between soft sponge cake with fruit jelly.
  - **Tiramisu Coupe / Chocolate Dad:** For chocolate lovers.
  - **Child & Baby friendly:** Vanilla scoop or pure fruit kulfi for the 6-year-old.
- **Family logistics:** Air-conditioned, bustling, high-speed turnover. If travelling with stroller, leave it in the Innova or fold at the entrance counter.

## Authentic Coastal Dining for Families

### 1. Machali (Near Sharavu Temple, Kadri)
- **Specialty:** Fresh catch coastal thalis, Anjal (Kingfish) tawa fry, squid sukka, prawn ghee roast.
- **Kid-friendly food:** Neer dosa (super soft, lacy rice crepes — baby can easily gum/eat these!), steamed rice with plain dal and dollop of ghee, tender coconut pudding.
- **Queue hack:** Arrive sharply at **12:30 PM** or **19:00 PM** to avoid the 30-minute peak crowd queue.

### 2. Giri Manja's (Car Street / Bhavathi)
- **Specialty:** Heritage ancestral home turned coastal seafood joint. Incredible tawa masala fish.
- **Atmosphere:** Traditional banana leaf service, cozy, authentic.
- **Family note:** Small premises; not stroller-friendly. Carrier recommended.

### 3. Hotel Janatha Deluxe / Woodlands (Pure Veg Breakfast)
- **Specialty:** Mangalore Buns (banana pooris with coconut chutney), Goli Baje (crisp mangalore pakoda), Filter Coffee, Khara Bath.
- **Baby friendly:** Mild steaming hot idli with fresh coconut milk or warm milk.

- **Clean Water:** Always ask for branded packaged water (Bisleri/Kinley) or boiled water for baby formula.
- **Hand Sanitizer:** Wipe down high chairs at Pabbas with baby-safe antibacterial wipes before seating.

## Pleasant Window
- **Afternoon Dessert Window (15:00–16:30 PM):** Pabbas is air-conditioned and uncrowded between 15:00 and 16:30, making it the perfect cool break between hotel rest and the evening beach!
