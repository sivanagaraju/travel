# Mangalore (city + IXE)

**Circuit role:** Must-cover **entry** (A/B/C/D/F) or **exit** (E).  
**Nights:** Usually **1** (Thu 12 on A). C adds Wed 11 as well.

## Pleasant

A **pleasant** Mangalore is **hotel → nap → one beach evening**, not a city temple crawl after a 05:35 flight. IXE is small; Innova should be **at arrivals**. Infant in the **carrier** or on a lap. Hotel with pool, restaurant, crib (The Ocean Pearl is the working example). Breakfast = idli/sambar, not a new fish fry.

## Peace

**Peace** is the hotel room 09:30–15:00 and Tannirbhavi’s tree park, not MG Road. Skip Kadri, St. Aloysius, and shopping on day one. The 6-year-old can do 30 minutes of pool with one adult while the infant sleeps.

## Handle kids

- Infant in the **carrier** or on a lap from the kerb.
- Stroller works in the hotel and on Tannirbhavi/Panambur sand-edge paths; not in temple queues if you add any.
- High chair: ask Ocean Pearl; have a lap plan.
- Hot water for bottles. Mosquito cream after 17:30 at the beach.

## Skip with a 1-year-old

Full-day “Mangalore sightseeing.” Ghat to Kukke **after 18:00**. Sunday evening Panambur.

## Calm vs crowded

City **3**. Hotel **2**. Beach evening Thursday **2–3**; Sunday **5**.
