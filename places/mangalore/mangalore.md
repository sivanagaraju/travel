# Mangalore (City & IXE Airport Gateway)

**Circuit role:** Must-cover **entry gateway** (Approaches A, B, C, D, F) or **exit terminal** (Approach E).  
**Stay duration:** **1 Night** (Thu 12 Nov on Approach A; Wed 11 Nov on Approach C).  
**Recommended base:** The Ocean Pearl (Kodialbail) or Ginger Mangalore.

## Pleasant Window
- **Arrival Morning (08:35–10:30):** After landing on IndiGo 6E 7581 at 08:35 AM, exit the terminal by 09:15 AM. Your hired Innova Crysta meets you at the arrivals porch.
- **Drive to Hotel (09:15–09:50):** 30-minute drive from the Bajpe table-top plateau into Mangalore city. Check into The Ocean Pearl, enjoy a hot breakfast, and freshen up.
- **Midday Recovery (10:30–15:30):** Protect the baby's rhythm! Feed the infant, let the baby take a long crib nap, and have a leisurely south Indian lunch (soft idli, curd rice, ghee dal) at the hotel restaurant. The 6-year-old can enjoy 30 minutes in the hotel swimming pool with one parent.
- **Evening Outing (16:30–18:30):** Pick **ONE** beach only: either [Tannirbhavi Beach](tannirbhavi-beach.md) (serene, casuarina trees) OR [Panambur Beach](panambur-beach.md) (active, camel rides).
- **Post-Beach Treat (19:00–20:00):** Stop at **Pabbas Ice Cream** on MG Road for their legendary Gadbad ice cream before an early 20:30 bedtime.

---

## Peace & Calm
- Mangalore city streets (Hampankatta, Falnir) can be noisy and traffic-choked between 17:00 and 19:30.
- **True family peace:** Found inside the shady casuarina woods of Tannirbhavi Tree Park, on the quiet terrace pool of your hotel, or inside the marble courtyard of Kudroli Temple after 19:00.
- **Rule:** Never attempt an intensive multi-temple city crawl on arrival day with an infant who woke up at 04:00 AM for the flight.

- **Baby Cot:** Request a baby crib/cot at The Ocean Pearl during room booking. If staying at Ginger, carry a travel bassinet.
- **Infant Sterilizing & Water:** Ask the hotel kitchen to provide a flask of rolling-boiled drinking water for preparing formula milk.
- **Mosquito Defense:** Coastal humidity brings evening mosquitoes near beaches and tree parks. Apply infant-safe bug lotion (patches or organic balm) from 17:00 onwards.
- **Child Food:** The 6-year-old will love Mangalore buns (mildly sweet, fluffy fried banana bread), neer dosa, and tender coconut water.

## What to Skip
- **Ghat drive to Kukke on Day 1 evening:** Strictly forbidden. Navigating the dark, winding Western Ghats after 18:00 with a crying infant is dangerous. Stay in Mangalore overnight and climb on Friday morning.
- **Kadri Manjunatha & Mangaladevi Temples on Day 1:** Don't overload the schedule; you have Kukke, Dharmasthala, and Udupi Krishna Matha ahead.
