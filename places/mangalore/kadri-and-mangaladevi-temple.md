**Circuit role:** Heritage & spiritual landmark in Mangalore city (10th-century hillside sanctuary with perennial freshwater springs, ancient Buddhist-Shaivite bronze sculptures, and the historic temple that gave Mangalore its name).

## Heritage Significance

Kadri Manjunatha Temple sits at the fascinating confluence of Mahayana Buddhism and Natha Shaivism. Dating back to at least 968 CE under the Alupa dynasty, the sanctum houses a breathtaking 1,000-year-old bronze triad featuring **Lokeshwara** (Bodhisattva Avalokiteshvara), hailed by historians as one of the finest bronze sculptures in Indian art. 

The perennial mountain springs cascade into nine freshwater bathing tanks where natural mountain water has flowed non-stop for over a millennium.
