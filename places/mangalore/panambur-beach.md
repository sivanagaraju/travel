# Panambur Beach, Mangalore

**Circuit role:** Alternative **Mangalore beach evening** option (pair with Tannirbhavi — do **ONE** well, never both on Day 1).  
**When to visit:** Approach A/B/D/F: **Thu 12 Nov 16:30–18:15**.  
**Distance:** ~13 km north of Mangalore city center (~25 min drive); ~14 km from IXE Airport (~25 min via NH66).

## Pleasant Window
- **16:30–17:15 (Carnival & Activity for 6yo):** Arrive by 16:30. The 6-year-old will be thrilled by the lively atmosphere: flying colorful kites, watching camels on the sand, and spotting massive cargo container vessels entering the New Mangalore Port channel.
- **17:15–18:00 (Sunset & Shore Walk):** Transition to the water edge where trained lifeguards monitor the designated safe swimming zone. Keep the infant in a supportive chest carrier.
- **18:00 (Exit before dusk rush):** Head back to the Innova before 18:30 to escape the exit road bottleneck.

---

## Peace & Noise Warning
- Panambur is Mangalore's most commercial beach. It features jet-skis, ATVs, camel rides, and loudspeaker announcements.
- **Recommendation:** If your infant is sensitive to noise, over-tired from the early morning flight, or prone to sensory overload, **choose Tannirbhavi instead**. Panambur is best only if the 6-year-old actively demands beach activities and rides.

- **Safety Perimeter:** Keep both children well away from the ATV and jet-ski launch lanes on the northern stretch of the beach.
- **Water Play Rule:** Ankles only for the 6-year-old under direct physical contact with one parent. The sea bed here slopes noticeably.
- **Baby Food Hygiene:** Do not buy open cut-fruit, ice golas, or fried street snacks for the baby. Stick to fresh tender coconut water cut directly in front of you.

---

## What This Place Covers
- Mangalore's primary coastal recreation hub with professional lifeguard coverage.
- Water sports zone (jet-ski, banana ride, parasailing — strictly for adults or older teenagers, skip for 6yo/baby).
- Direct views of New Mangalore Port ships and lighthouse.

---

## What to Skip
- **Water sports:** Not suitable for a 1-year-old infant or 6-year-old child.
- **Late-night staying:** The beach plaza gets rowdy after 19:00 on weekends.
