# Panambur Beach

**Circuit role:** Must-cover **Mangalore evening** option (do Panambur **or** Tannirbhavi, not a exhausted double).  
**When:** Same slots as Tannirbhavi. Closer to **IXE** (~12–15 km).

## Pleasant

A **pleasant** Panambur visit is a **short** weekday evening: sandcastles, a coconut, watch the port lights. It has more stalls, more sports, more people than Tannirbhavi. That can delight a 6-year-old and overstimulate a one-year-old. Cap at 90 minutes.

## Peace

**Peace** is not Panambur’s brand. If the infant is already tired, **choose Tannirbhavi**. If the 6-year-old needs “a real beach with things happening,” Panambur Thursday is fine; **Sunday evening is a 5** — avoid.

## Handle kids

- Stay in the lifeguard zone. Buoys and sports gear are for older kids.
- Hold hands near jet-ski paths.
- Street food: adults only on night one. Children: banana, biscuit, hotel dinner after.

## What this place covers

Main city beach · stalls · water sports (not for this infant) · sunset · parking crush on weekends.

## Skip

Parasailing, late-night shacks, combining with Tannirbhavi the same evening.

## Calm vs crowded

Thu **3**. Sat **4**. Sun evening **5**.
