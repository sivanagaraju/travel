## Pleasant Window
- **09:00–10:00:** Early morning is cool, quiet, and illuminated by soft morning light streaming through stained glass windows.
- Painted in 1899–1900 by Italian Jesuit Brother Antonio Moscheni, the entire ceiling and interior walls are covered with breathtaking frescoes and canvas murals depicting biblical stories.

---

## Peace & Atmosphere
- St. Aloysius Chapel is a sanctuary of profound quiet.
- It offers a calm, climate-controlled retreat from coastal humidity.
- The 6-year-old child will be captivated spotting painted angels, apostles, and historic details across the ceiling arches.

---

## Handling Kids & Baby Logistics
- **Stroller Accessible:** Has a level ramp leading into the side transept. Strollers roll smoothly down the wide central carpeted nave.
- **Noise Rule:** It is an active heritage chapel; gentle whispers only. If the infant cries, step out to the breezy shaded college garden veranda immediately.
- **Photography:** No flash photography inside to protect 120-year-old oil and water-based pigments.

---

## What This Place Covers
- 100% frescoed Italian renaissance chapel.
- Quiet 30–40 minute cultural experience.
- Small campus museum exhibiting ancient coins, maps, and Mangalorean artifacts.
