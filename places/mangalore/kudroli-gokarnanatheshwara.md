# Kudroli Gokarnanatheshwara Temple, Mangalore

**Circuit role:** Optional **evening spiritual & architectural stop** in Mangalore (ideal for families seeking a clean, brightly illuminated, stroller-friendly temple).  
**When to visit:** Approach A/B/D/F: **Thu 12 Nov 18:30–19:30** (after beach or before dinner).  
**Location:** Kudroli, ~3 km from Mangalore city center / The Ocean Pearl hotel (~10 min drive).

## Pleasant Window
- **18:30–19:30 (Illumination Hour):** The temple is renowned for its magnificent evening illumination. Thousands of golden fairy lights illuminate the Chola-style gopura and water fountains.
- The polished marble courtyard stays cool after dark, making it comfortable for toddlers to walk barefoot without scorching their feet.

---

## Peace & Atmosphere
- Built in 1912 by Sri Narayana Guru advocating social equality for all communities, this temple has an unusually inclusive, welcoming atmosphere.
- Unlike ancient cramped sanctums, the open courtyard is wide and airy. If the infant is irritable, one parent can stroll with the baby around the illuminated fountains while the other parent and 6-year-old take darshan.

- **Dress Code:** Relaxed and welcoming compared to Udupi or Gokarna. Modest casual Indian attire is fine (jeans/t-shirts, churidars). Men are **not** required to remove shirts in the outer courtyard.
- **Darshan Duration:** 15–20 minutes. Queue moves continuously; no crushing or aggressive pushes.

---

## What This Place Covers
- Grand Gokarnanatheshwara Shiva Linga and Navadurga idols.
- Spectacular illuminated water fountains and temple tank with Shiva statue holding the Ganga.
- Relaxing, peaceful 45-minute family stroll.

---

## What to Skip
- Visiting during afternoon hours (12:30–16:00) when sanctum doors are closed and marble floor gets warm.
- Mangalore Dasara period (Sep/Oct) when 100,000+ devotees visit daily (your Nov dates are post-Dasara, completely peaceful).
