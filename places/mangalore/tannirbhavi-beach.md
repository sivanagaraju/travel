# Tannirbhavi Beach

**Circuit role:** Must-cover **Mangalore evening** option (pair with Panambur: do **one** well).  
**When:** Approach A/B/D/F **Thu 12 Nov 16:30–18:30**. C also Wed 11. E: **morning 07:00–09:00 on 18 Nov**, not evening.

## Pleasant

A **pleasant** Tannirbhavi hour is late afternoon on a **weekday**: wide sand, casuarina shade, a tree park the 6-year-old can walk without jet-ski noise. Water is relatively shallow near the shore. Karnataka Tourism lists lifeguards, toilets, eateries, benches.

## Peace

More **peace** than Panambur. Sit under the trees, not in a sports cluster. If a kite festival or Sunday crowd is on, switch to the hotel pool — that is still a valid “Mangalore beach day” for an infant.

## Handle kids

- **Ankles only.** One adult touching the child in water.
- Stroller to the edge; sand eats wheels — then carrier.
- Toilets exist; still pack wipes.
- Leave before full dark. No night swim.

## What this place covers

Beach · 15-acre tree park · toilets · parking · simple snacks · ferry from Sultan Battery (skip with infant unless you enjoy extra handling).

## Skip

Deep wading, inflatable toys beyond knee depth, sunset-plus-shopping.

## Calm vs crowded

Thu evening **2–3**. Sunday evening **4–5**. Morning on E **2**.
