# Tannirbhavi Beach & Tree Park, Mangalore

**Circuit role:** **Top Pick for Mangalore Beach Evening** (quieter and greener than Panambur).  
**When to visit:** Approach A/B/D/F: **Thu 12 Nov 16:30–18:15**. Approach C: **Wed 11 Nov**. Approach E: **Wed 18 Nov morning (07:30–09:00)**.  
**Distance:** ~9 km from Mangalore city center (~20 min drive); ~16 km from IXE Airport (~30 min).

## Pleasant Window
- **16:30–17:30 (Tree Park & Play):** Enter the 15-acre **Tannirbhavi Tree Park** first. Wheel the stroller along the paved paths under towering casuarina pines. The 6-year-old can run across open lawns and play on swings, while the baby enjoys gentle sea breezes in shaded comfort.
- **17:30–18:15 (Sunset on Sand):** Leave the stroller locked in the Innova or carry it to the concrete promenade edge. Transfer the infant to a chest carrier. Walk onto the golden sand to watch the sunset over the Arabian Sea.
- **18:15 (Pack & Exit):** Pack up before twilight fades completely. This avoids mosquitoes and guarantees an effortless drive back into the city.

---

## Peace & Atmosphere
- Tannirbhavi is distinctly more tranquil and scenic than commercial beaches. There are **no deafening jet-skis or loudspeaker shacks**.
- The soothing rustle of casuarina needles combined with rhythmic ocean waves provides a deeply calming environment for an over-stimulated infant after a flight day.
- **Insider Tip:** Avoid taking the Sultan Battery boat ferry with a baby and stroller. Driving directly via the Tannirbhavi Beach Road over the bridge is far more comfortable and private.

- **Baby Feeding Spot:** Benches inside the Tree Park provide private, shaded spots for nursing or bottle-feeding away from beach crowds.
- **Footwear:** Sand gets cool by 16:30. Barefoot or flip-flops for parents; light water shoes or crocs for the 6-year-old.
- **Mosquito Guard:** Tree parks attract evening bugs after 17:45. Apply baby-safe mosquito patches or citronella spray to clothes.

---

## What This Place Covers
- 15-acre Karnataka Forest Department Tree Park with herbal garden and statues.
- Broad, clean sandy shoreline with active lifeguard watchtowers.
- Shaded seating areas and snack kiosks (fresh tender coconut, roasted sweet corn).
- Safe family sunset viewing.

---

## What to Skip
- **Sultan Battery Ferry Boat:** Carrying a stroller and baby onto a crowded wooden boat is unnecessary hassle when a direct road exists.
- **Deep wading or swimming:** Coastal currents here are deceptively strong; keep water play strictly at ankle-depth for the 6-year-old.
