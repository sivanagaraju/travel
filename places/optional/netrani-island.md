# Netrani Island (Pigeon Island) — Scuba & Snorkeling

**Circuit role:** **Strictly off-limits excursion for this family** (A tiny heart-shaped coral island in the Arabian Sea located 19 km off the coast of Murudeshwar, famous as India's premier mainland scuba diving destination).  
**Family verdict:** **100% STRICT SKIP.** Legally restricted and physically dangerous for infants and children under 10 years.

## Why Netrani is Prohibited for Families with Toddlers

1. **Zero Landing Capability:**
   - Netrani Island is an uninhabited jagged rock formation. There is **no beach to disembark onto, no shade, no toilets, and no shelter**.
   - Visitors must remain on board a bobbing, swaying boat for 4 to 5 continuous hours in direct open sunlight while divers take turns in the water.
2. **Age Restrictions:**
   - Certified dive operators (such as *Netrani Adventures* and *West Coast Water Sports*) enforce a strict minimum age of **10 to 12 years** for introductory discover scuba dives and snorkeling.
   - Infants and toddlers under 5 years are strictly barred from boarding the speedboats for maritime liability reasons.
3. **Emergency Medical Isolation:**
   - The island is located over an hour away from the mainland by boat. If an infant develops heat exhaustion, dehydration, or an allergic reaction, emergency evacuation across choppy open sea is extremely difficult.

Enjoy the dramatic views of the 123-foot Lord Shiva statue from the mainland Raja Gopura lift, and save scuba diving for when your children are teenagers!
