# Jog Falls (Gerosoppa Falls) — Feasibility Analysis

**Circuit role:** **Optional inland detour — NOT recommended on the 7-day coastal calendar** (India's second-highest plunge waterfall, dropping 253 meters / 830 feet in four distinct cascades: Raja, Roarer, Rocket, and Rani).  
**Driving distance:** ~60 km east of Honnavar (~1.5 hours uphill driving via NH69 Sharavathi Ghat road); ~100 km from Gokarna (~2.5 hours).  
**Family verdict:** **SKIP on the 12–18 Nov itinerary.** In mid-November, the Sharavathi River flow is severely reduced by the Linganamakki hydroelectric dam, making the 3-hour round-trip mountain detour disproportionate to the visual payoff for a family with a 1-year-old infant.

## The November Water Volume Reality

1. **Dam-Regulated Flow:**
   - Jog Falls is fed by the **Linganamakki Dam**, operated by the Karnataka Power Corporation Limited (KPCL).
   - During the monsoon peaks (July to September), excess dam water is released, creating a thunderous, misty white wall of water across the entire 830-foot gorge.
   - **By mid-November**, the dam gates are closed to store water for hydroelectric power generation. The waterfall shrinks to thin, slender white ribbons trickling down the massive black rock face, except on Sunday afternoons or national holidays when limited water is released for tourists.
2. **The 1,400 Steps Trap:**
   - Guidebooks and tourist blogs often boast about "climbing down to the bottom of the gorge".
   - The descent involves **1,400 steep stone steps** down a vertical cliff face with no lift or return cable car.
   - Walking down and back up with an infant in a chest carrier takes over 2.5 hours of punishing knee-grinding effort in humid heat. **Strictly avoid.**

---

## Why You Should Skip Jog Falls on This Trip

- Inserting Jog Falls on Saturday 14 Nov would add **120 km and 3+ hours of mountain ghat driving** onto a day that already includes Udupi, Maravanthe, Murudeshwar, and Gokarna.
- It would force the family to drive the dark forest ghat roads well past 20:00 PM, violating the core safety rule: *No ghat driving after 18:00 PM*.

---

## Verdict

Save Jog Falls for a future monsoon-season trip in August/September when the dam floodgates are open, and dedicate your Saturday to the glorious coastal sunset at Kasarkod Eco Beach and reaching Gokarna in daylight!

## Pleasant Window
- **Morning Gallery View (09:00–10:30 AM):** If ever visiting, early morning from the KSTDC viewing gallery offers cool temperatures before midday mist burns off.

---

## Kids / Baby Logistics
- **Viewing Gallery (Stroller OK):** The upper KSTDC viewing deck is paved and stroller friendly.
- **1,400 Steps Ban:** Never attempt the 1,400 stone steps with an infant carrier.
