# Optional & Off-Calendar Excursions

Detailed feasibility guides for famous Karnataka attractions outside the core 12–18 Nov family calendar, explaining why each is either optional or strictly skipped with young children.

---

## Optional Excursions Directory

| Destination | Category | Driving from Circuit | Family Verdict for 1yo & 6yo | Reason & Summary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Dandeli Wildlife & Kali River](dandeli.md) | Jungle & Wildlife | ~85 km from Gokarna | **SKIP on 7-Day** | 4-hr open jeep safaris & rafting unsafe for infant; needs separate 2-night trip |
| [Netrani Island Scuba](netrani-island.md) | Coral Marine Reserve | 19 km offshore from Murudeshwar | **STRICT PROHIBITION** | Open-sea speedboats, zero beach landing, minimum age 10+ |
| [Jog Falls](jog-falls.md) | Inland Waterfall | 60 km up Sharavathi ghat from Honnavar | **SKIP in November** | Dam-regulated seasonal trickle; 1,400 steep steps; adds 3 h mountain drive |
