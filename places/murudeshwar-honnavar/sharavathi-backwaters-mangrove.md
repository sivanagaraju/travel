# Sharavathi Backwaters & Kandla Van Mangrove Boardwalk (Honnavar)

**Circuit role:** Highly recommended **nature oasis & calm backwater experience** (Karnataka's premier mangrove ecosystem featuring an elevated wooden suspension boardwalk (*Kandla Van*) and tranquil 45-minute eco-boat cruises along the emerald backwaters of the Sharavathi River).  
**When to visit:**  
- **Approach F:** **Sun 15 Nov 10:30–11:45 AM** (The ultimate morning slow-travel experience before heading to Gokarna for the birthday).  
- **Approach A / B / C:** **Sat 14 Nov ~16:45–17:30** (Alternative to Kasarkod Eco Beach or quick 30-min boardwalk walk).  
- **Approach E:** **Mon 16 Nov 10:30–11:45 AM**.  
**Driving distance:** ~28 km north of Murudeshwar (~30 min drive); situated immediately alongside the Sharavathi River Bridge in Honnavar. Located 2 km off NH66.  
**Hours & Tickets:** Boardwalk open daily 08:30–18:00. Boardwalk entry: ₹15–₹20 per adult. Backwater boat ride: ₹200–₹350 per seat (or ₹1,200–₹1,500 for private 8-seater motorboat).

## Why Honnavar Backwaters Win Over Open Sea for Families

Travel vlogs consistently highlight Honnavar's backwaters as the hidden gem of the entire Karnataka coast. For families with infants and young children, it offers critical safety advantages over open-ocean boating:

1. **Zero Ocean Swell / Zero Sea Sickness:**
   - The Sharavathi backwaters are enclosed by coastal islands and riverbanks. The water surface is mirror-calm.
   - There are no rolling ocean waves, sudden crests, or spray, ensuring the 1-year-old infant remains completely relaxed without nausea or fear.
2. **Kandla Van Wooden Boardwalk:**
   - Built by the Karnataka Forest Department, this elevated wooden path snakes deep into a rare mangrove swamp (*Rhizophora* and *Avicennia* trees).
   - Solid timber handrails line both sides, preventing any accidental falls.
   - Paved planks allow a compact stroller to roll across the main boardwalk sections, letting parents immerse in nature while pushing the baby.
3. **Wildlife Engagement for 6-Year-Old Child:**
   - Children can peer down between the mangrove aerial stilt roots to spot thousands of tiny blue fiddler crabs, mudskippers walking on water, and colorful kingfishers diving for fish.

## Handling Kids & Baby Logistics

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                            BABY CARE ON SITE                                |
|                                                                             |
|  [ Certified Life Jackets ]   [ Boardwalk Safety ]     [ Private Boat Hire ]|
|  - Compulsory life vests      - Stroller rolls easily  - Hire private boat  |
|    provided. Hold infant in     on boardwalk; carry      for ₹1,200 so you  |
|    lap with parent vest on.     toddler at jetty ramp.   have full control. |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

1. **Boat Boarding Protocol:**
   - When stepping from the floating jetty onto the boat, parents must wear the infant in a chest carrier or hand the infant to the second parent already seated securely on board.
   - **Private Boat Recommendation:** For just ₹1,200–₹1,500, hire an entire 8-seater covered boat for your family. This allows you to ask the captain to drive at gentle trolling speeds, stop whenever the baby wants to nurse, and turn back early if needed.
2. **Sun & Insect Protection:**
   - While the mangrove forest is shaded, apply baby-safe natural insect repellent cream on exposed ankles and arms.
   - Keep a sunhat on the 6-year-old child during the open river stretches of the boat cruise.

## What This Place Covers

- 400-meter elevated Kandla Van mangrove wooden boardwalk.
- 45-minute calm backwater motorboat cruise to Mavinkurve island channel.
- Exceptional birdwatching and coastal mangrove biodiversity education.
- Scenic views of the iconic Sharavathi railway and highway bridges.

---

## What to Skip

- Speedboats offering fast high-octane spins on the river: Choose the traditional slow wooden motor launch instead.
- Disembarking onto unpaved island sandbars with an infant.
