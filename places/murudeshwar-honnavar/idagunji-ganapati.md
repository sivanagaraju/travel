# Idagunji Ganapati (Mahaganapati Temple)

**Circuit role:** Must-cover **short, child-friendly darshan**.  
**When:** **A/B/C Sat 14 ~16:20–16:40** (evening window **15:00–20:30**, **after** Murudeshwar). **F Sun 15 ~08:00–09:00** (morning window **06:00–13:00**). **E Mon 16 ~12:00** before 13:00 close. Official: [idagunjidevaru.com](http://www.idagunjidevaru.com/details.php).

## Pleasant

A **pleasant** Idagunji is **10 minutes**: standing two-handed Ganapati, coconut offering, courtyard green. It sits **~20.5 km north of Murudeshwar**, ~5–7 km west of NH66, ~14 km from Honnavar town. Do **not** visit it *before* Murudeshwar when you are driving north — that is a backtrack.

## Peace

Quieter than Kukke or Murudeshwar. Reviewers mention even festival lines moving in 30–40 min; you want **10**. **Peace** is the forest-edge village, not a theme park.

## Handle kids

- 10-minute darshan is the point. Ganapati is easy for a 6-year-old.
- Closed **13:00–15:00**. Approach A used to hit that gap by mistake; the linear order **fixes** it.
- Free noon bhojana at Mayura Prasada if you are there at lunch (**F** morning is too early; **E** might catch it).
- Stroller in the courtyard; carrier in the sanctum if crowded.

## What this place covers

Sanctum · coconut ritual · optional prasadam · **not** a long seva list for this trip.

## Skip

Waiting 40 minutes for a “better” slot. Photograph the facade and go if the infant is done.

## Calm vs crowded

Weekday-style **2**. Ganesh Chaturthi **5** (you are in November — fine).
