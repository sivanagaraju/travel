# Eco Beach, Kasarkod (Honnavar)

**Circuit role:** Must-cover **managed eco-park & Blue Flag certified beach** (Karnataka's pristine, international Blue Flag certified family beach featuring paved stroller walkways, dense casuarina pine tree groves, modern clean washrooms, and dedicated children's play zones).  
**When to visit:**  
- **Approach A / B / C:** **Sat 14 Nov ~17:00–17:45 (Strict exit by 18:00 to reach Gokarna safely)**.  
- **Approach F:** **Sun 15 Nov ~09:15–10:30 (Peak unhurried morning calm)**.  
- **Approach E:** **Mon 16 Nov late morning / noon**.  
**Driving distance:** ~25 km north of Murudeshwar (~25 min drive); ~3 km south of Honnavar bridge; ~50 km south of Gokarna. Located 1.5 km west of NH66.  
**Entry & Hours:** Open daily 08:30–18:00 (Park gates lock at 18:30). Entry ticket: ~₹10–₹20 per adult; children under 3 enter free. Vehicle parking: ₹30.

## The Blue Flag Eco Distinction

Kasarkod Eco Beach is one of only a select few beaches in India awarded the prestigious **Blue Flag Certification** by the Foundation for Environmental Education (FEE), Denmark. 

To maintain this global rating, the park complies with 33 rigorous criteria covering water quality, environmental management, safety services, and universal accessibility. Unlike chaotic public beaches, you will find zero commercial hawkers, zero open garbage, clean solar-powered lighting, grey-water recycling plants, and strict single-use plastic restrictions.

## Pleasant Window

- **Late Afternoon (17:00–17:45 PM on Approach A):**
  - The sun descends toward the Arabian Sea horizon, filtering golden rays through the dense casuarina needles.
  - The temperature drops to a balmy 25°C with a refreshing sea breeze.
  - Perfect for a 45-minute revitalizing pause: the 6-year-old child can expend energy on the playground swings, while parents change the 1-year-old infant's diaper in the clean restrooms and nurse them in a shaded gazebo.
- **Strict Departure Rule for Approach A:**
  - You **must leave the park gate by 17:45–18:00 PM**! The drive from Honnavar to Gokarna takes ~55 minutes on NH66. Leaving by 18:00 ensures you cross the Sharavathi River bridge in twilight and arrive in Gokarna by 19:00 PM without stressful night driving.
- **Morning Window (09:15–10:30 AM on Approach F):**
  - For families on Approach F who slept at Murudeshwar, Sunday morning at Kasarkod is an unhurried paradise: empty benches, quiet bird calls, and gentle morning surf.

1. **Why Kasarkod is a Parent's Lifesaver on the Northbound Drive:**
   - After visiting the crowded Kanduka Hill at Murudeshwar and the temple at Idagunji, Kasarkod provides the ultimate sanitization and comfort stop.
   - The modern restrooms are among the cleanest public facilities in Karnataka, making it the premier location to wash hands, change clothes, and freshen up before Gokarna.
2. **Stroller & Pram Accessibility:**
   - Wide, barrier-free paved paths wind throughout the pine park and lead to the wooden beach boardwalk.
   - You can push the stroller smoothly from the car door right to the edge of the sand without lifting it.
3. **Food & Water Canteen:**
   - The park has a small eco-canteen serving bottled mineral water, hot tea, biscuits, and packaged coconut snacks.
   - No heavy greasy food stalls are allowed inside the park, keeping the grounds exceptionally clean.

## What This Place Covers

- Paved casuarina park with wooden gazebos and shaded benches.
- Children's eco-playground with slides, swings, and sand play.
- Pristine Blue Flag certified beach shoreline.
- Spotless western toilets and fresh-water washing taps.

---

## What to Skip

- **Deep ocean swimming:** Despite lifeguards, the open Arabian Sea here has strong undercurrents. Keep toddlers and children strictly in the shallow ankle wash.
- **Apsarakonda Waterfall steps if pressed for time:** If you arrive after 17:00 PM on Approach A, skip the stairs of Apsarakonda and spend all your time relaxing inside the Kasarkod Eco Beach park before driving north.
