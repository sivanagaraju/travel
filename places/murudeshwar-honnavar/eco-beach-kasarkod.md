# Eco Beach, Kasarkod (Honnavar)

**Circuit role:** Must-cover **managed park beach** — toilets, shade, play, the anti-Murudeshwar-plaza.  
**When:** **A/B/C Sat 14 ~16:55–17:40, out by 18:00**. **F Sun 15 ~09:15–10:15** (emptiest). **E Mon 16 late morning**. North of Murudeshwar (~24–27 km). ~3 km from Honnavar.

## Pleasant

A **pleasant** Eco Beach is a **fenced, shaded, sit-down** beach: benches, play area, pay toilets, canteen, casuarinas. Hours published as **08:00–18:00** or **08:30–18:30** — **treat 18:00 as last exit**. Tickets **₹10/₹5** or **₹20, under-3 free** — **pay at the gate**. Under-1 is free either way.

## Peace

This is the **peace** beach of the Honnavar strip. Not a party shore. Birdy in winter mornings (**F** Sunday 09:00 is gold). If you only have 30 minutes on Approach A, still go: toilets + 6-year-old runs + infant shade.

## Handle kids

- Stroller **works** here (paths, park).
- Play area for the 6-year-old; infant on a mat under shade.
- Water: park edge only, same ankle rule.
- **Out by 18:00** on Saturday or you are pushing Gokarna into night. Do **not** drive south to Murudeshwar after this.

## What this place covers

Managed beach · toilets · shade · play · canteen · parking · Blue Flag / eco-park story. **Not** Apsarakonda waterfall (extra steps, skip with infant).

## Skip

Arriving at 17:50. Combining with Jog Falls. Assuming “no ticket” blogs — bring ₹100 cash.

## Calm vs crowded

Sat 17:00 **1–2**. Sun 09:00 **1**. Holiday noon **3**.
