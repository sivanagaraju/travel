# Murudeshwar Temple and Beach

**Circuit role:** Must-cover **123 ft / 37 m Shiva** + temple on Kanduka hill, sea on three sides.  
**When:** **A/B/C Sat 14 15:00–16:00** (sanctum back after the midday close). **D Sun 15 15:00–16:00**. **F Sat 14 15:00–sunset + overnight**. **E Mon 16 15:00–16:00** southbound. **Never 11:00–15:00.**

## Pleasant

A **pleasant** Murudeshwar is **one hour after 15:00**: statue photos, short darshan, optional gopuram lift **₹10–20**, out. The statue is designed to catch light; late afternoon is enough. Overnight on **F** can add night illumination — book a hotel **away from the plaza noise**.

## Peace

The plaza is **not peace**. **Peace** is the view from the lift or a bench **behind** the crush, or the car after 16:00. Main beach at the statue feet is a **5** at midday and **not a toddler swim** any hour you will be there.

## Handle kids

- Hold the 6-year-old at the railings. Cliff + crowd.
- Lift: only if the queue is short and you will still hit Eco Beach before 18:00 on A.
- Beach in front of Shiva: photograph, **no swim**.
- Modest clothes over beachwear for the sanctum.
- Infant: carrier. Stroller dies on the approach.
