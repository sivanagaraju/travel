## Sourcing the 1st Birthday Cake (Action Checklist)

Gokarna is a small pilgrimage and beach town; high-end European bakeries are scarce. Sourcing a fresh, hygienic, and photogenic 1st birthday cake requires pre-planning:

### Recommended Bakeries & Options

1. **Option 1 (Best & Safest): Kudle Beach View Resort In-House Pastry Chef**
   - **Procedure:** Email / WhatsApp the resort management at least **7–10 days before arrival** (by 5 Nov 2026).
   - **Request:** 1 kg fresh eggless chocolate truffle, vanilla sponge, or fresh fruit gateau with personalized lettering: *"Happy 1st Birthday [Baby's Name]!"*.
   - **Advantage:** Stored in resort walk-in refrigeration until 18:30 PM; zero risk of melting during road transit.
2. **Option 2: Iyengar Bakery / City Bakes, Gokarna Town**
   - Located on Car Street / Main Road in Gokarna town (~12 min drive from Kudle).
   - Good for traditional sponge cakes, fresh pineapple cakes, and simple plum/vanilla cakes.
   - Order on Saturday afternoon upon arrival, pick up Sunday 16:30 PM.
3. **Option 3: Kumta Town Premium Bakery (25 km south via Innova)**
   - Kumta is a larger commercial hub with established cake shops (*Modern Bakers*, *Cake Paradise*).
   - If a multi-tiered designer themed cake is desired, have your Innova driver pick it up from Kumta on Sunday afternoon while the baby naps.

1. **Infant Water Security:**
   - Never use tap water for infant formula or feeding bottle washes.
   - Use the electric kettle in your resort room to bring bottled mineral water to a rolling boil for 3 minutes before cooling it in a clean thermos.
2. **Toddler Meal Orders at Cafes:**
   - Restaurant kitchens in coastal towns use generous green chillies and coconut paste.
   - Always specify to the waiter: *"For a 1-year-old baby: Bilkul mirchi nahi, namak bohot kam"* (Zero chilli, very low salt).
   - Plain curd rice (*Mosaranna*), steamed plain dosa, and mashed bananas are universally available and gentle on little tummies.
3. **Emergency Pediatric Pharmacy in Gokarna:**
   - Gokarna town has reliable pharmacies along Main Road (*Sri Sai Medicals* and *Government Hospital Road Chemists*).
   - Keep a basic emergency pouch in your daypack: infant paracetamol drops (Calpol/Crocin), oral rehydration salts (Electral), enterogermina, and anti-colic drops.
