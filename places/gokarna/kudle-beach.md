# Kudle Beach, Gokarna

**Circuit role:** Must-cover **family beach** and **birthday-base shoreline**.  
**When:** **A Sun 15 07:00–10:00** and other Gokarna mornings. Sleep **above Kudle** (pool + sit-out for cake).

## Pleasant

A **pleasant** Kudle morning is the crescent: cleaner and calmer than Gokarna Main, TripAdvisor families calling it **clean and safe for children** if you stay shallow. Good sunrise/sunset. This is where the 6-year-old should **play**, and where the infant should **sit in shade**, not Om at peak.

## Peace

Kudle is the **peace** beach of Gokarna for this family. Fewer party clusters than Om. Night sand is quiet; still go back to the hotel to sleep. Approach road is **steep** — ask the hotel how they move a stroller the last 200 m (buggy/taxi).

## Handle kids

- Shallow only. One adult in the water line.
- Shade is scarce — hotel umbrella / shack.
- Steep walk: **carrier** down, stroller useless on the path from some hotels.
- Pool at the resort is the infant’s **safest water** on the whole trip. Birthday cake on the **private sit-out**, not on the public sand.
- If no autos at sunset with kids, don’t get stuck on the beach at dusk — hotel transfer number in the phone.

## What this place covers

Kudle sand · sunset point · shacks · resort pool (if you booked it) · **birthday shoreline**. Not Om, not the temple.

## Skip

Trekking to Om along the cliff with a stroller. Party hostels. SwaSwara (wrong beach policy for young children anyway).

## Calm vs crowded

Sun 07:00 **2**. Sun 17:00 **3**. Mon morning **1–2**.
