**Circuit role:** Cultural & spiritual heritage stop (Sacred 6-acre man-made freshwater temple tank surrounded by ancient agraharas, mathas, and palm trees in central Gokarna).  
**When to visit:**  
- **Approach A / B / C / F:** **Mon 16 Nov 06:45–07:45 AM** (serene early morning walk immediately before or after Mahabaleshwar Temple).  
- **Approach E:** **Sat 14 Nov early morning**.  
**Location:** Situated in the center of Gokarna old town, ~350 meters east of Sri Mahabaleshwar Temple and ~10 min drive from Kudle Beach.

## Heritage Significance

*Kotitirtha* translates literally to **"A Crore Sacred Springs"**. 

According to regional lore, saintly king Garuda dropped holy water collected from thousands of sacred rivers across India into this lake, ensuring that a ritual ablution here carries the spiritual merit of bathing in all sacred tirthas simultaneously. 

Surrounded by century-old heritage homes (*agraharas*) with carved wooden doorways and terracotta-tiled sloping roofs, Kotitirtha preserves the unhurried, timeless Vedic atmosphere of ancient Gokarna.

---

## Pleasant Window

- **Early Dawn (06:30–07:45 AM) is Magical:**
  - Golden dawn light reflects off the emerald-green water.
  - Temple bells chime softly from surrounding shrines, and priests in traditional attire carry sacred brass water pots.
  - The temperature is crisp (22°C–24°C), and the streets are blissfully free of vehicle traffic.
  - An easy 45-minute stroller stroll around the perimeter before the town stirs.
- **Why Avoid Midday:**
  - The stone banks become hot, and midday shadows diminish the photographic beauty of the water reflections.

1. **Water Safety Strict Rule:**
   - The stone ghat steps descend straight into deep lake water without guard railings.
   - **Do not let children play unattended near the water's edge.**
   - Keep the 1-year-old infant securely inside the stroller or in a chest carrier while admiring the lake from the upper paved promenade.
2. **Sri Bhadrakali Temple Blessings:**
   - Located at the southern entrance of Kotitirtha.
   - Traditionally, families visiting Gokarna with newborns and young children offer a simple coconut to Goddess Bhadrakali to pray for the child's health and protection from negative energies (*Drishti*).
   - Darshan takes less than 5 minutes.

## What to Skip

- Immersing the infant in the lake water: While pilgrims perform ritual baths, lake water is stagnant and not suitable for infant skin.
- Visiting on Sunday evenings when local prayer groups crowd the ghats.
