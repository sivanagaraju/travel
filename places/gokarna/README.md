# Gokarna Regional Circuit & Birthday Base

Gokarna is the anchor destination of this family journey—the multi-night base where the infant celebrates his **1st Birthday on Sunday, 15 November 2026**. It balances ancient temple sanctity with some of India's most breathtaking, secluded coves.
