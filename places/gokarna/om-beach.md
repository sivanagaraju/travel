# Om Beach, Gokarna

**Circuit role:** Must-cover **cove beach**.  
**When:** **A Sun 15 15:00–16:30** optional if naps survived, and/or **Mon 16**. Same on B/C/F. **E** can use Fri–Sat here before the birthday.

## Pleasant

A **pleasant** Om is the **cove shape**: water feels calmer than Gokarna Main, road access (unlike Half Moon/Paradise), family photos, Namaste Café if you must, **knees-max** in the sea. Sunday afternoon is busier than Kudle morning — keep it to 90 minutes on the birthday.

## Peace

Om is more “known” than Kudle. **Peace** is the far arm of the cove, or **don’t come at 16:00 on Sunday**. Birthday **peace** belongs at the **resort sit-out**, not a shack queue. Namaste Café is classic and **not** the cake venue unless the resort kitchen fails.

## Handle kids

- Road drop, then a **non-stroller-friendly** bit to the sand. **Carrier + one backpack.**
- Cove ≠ pool. Still Arabian Sea.
- No Half Moon / Paradise with a stroller and a one-year-old (boat or trek).
- 6-year-old: sand play. Infant: shade cloth; Om has less tree shade than you want.

## What this place covers

Om cove · shacks · road access · photos. **Does not** cover Half Moon, Paradise, or the temple (those are separate musts/skips).

## Skip

Water sports aimed at groups, late-night Om with an infant, SwaSwara (children under 10 / adults-only).

## Calm vs crowded

Mon morning **2**. Sun afternoon **3**. Main Beach next door **4–5** — skip Main.
