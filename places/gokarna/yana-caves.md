# Yana Caves (optional)

**Circuit role:** **Optional.** Not a must-cover.  
**When:** Approach **A/C/F Tue 17 Nov** only if the baby is having a good week. **Skip on B.** ~**48–52 km** from Gokarna, **1 h 20 m–1 h 30 m** each way, then a **short forest walk / steps**.

## Pleasant

A **pleasant** Yana is a **cool November morning**: karst towers, 30–45 min walk, tea after. Entry blogs list ~₹5 + parking ~₹40 — confirm at the booth. Hours often **08:00–18:00**. **Leave by 16:00** so the forest road is not dusk.

## Peace

This is optional **peace** in the ghats, not a beach. If the infant fought the Friday temple belt, **stay at Kudle**. Missing Yana does not break the trip.

## Handle kids

- **No stroller.** Infant **carrier**. 6-year-old in closed shoes.
- Round trip **~96–104 km + walk** — FLAG vs 2.5 h if you count the outing as a driving day.
- Elderly knees / infant nap: skip.
- Not on the Hubli exit road in a way that makes 18 Nov safer — it is a **side** trip. Don’t sandwich it into the FLAG Hubli morning.

## What this place covers

Bhairaveshwara / karst · short walk · tiny cave shrine. **Not** a full-day trek (ignore 8 km trek blogs unless you are a different family).

## Skip

Monsoon leech season (you are in November — usually OK). Forcing it on the 6-day. Combining with Dandeli.

## Calm vs crowded

Tue morning **2**. Holiday noon **3**.
