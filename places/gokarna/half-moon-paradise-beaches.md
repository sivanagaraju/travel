# Half Moon Beach & Paradise Beach (Gokarna South)

**Circuit role:** **Skip the overland trek / Consider scenic boat cruise only** (Two isolated, pristine coves nestled between rocky cliffs south of Om Beach, inaccessible by road).  
**When to visit:**  
- **Approach A / B / C / F:** If desiring a 30-minute coastal cruise, board a licensed boat from **Om Beach on Mon 16 Nov morning (08:30–10:00)**.  
- **Overland Hiking Trail:** **100% STRICT SKIP FOR THIS FAMILY**.  
**Access:** Zero road access. Accessible only via steep cliff hiking trails or motorboats from Om Beach.

## Master Comparison: Trek vs Boat Cruise for a Family with Kids

| Dimension | Option 1: Overland Cliff Trek | Option 2: 30-Min Scenic Coastal Motorboat |
|---|---|---|
| **Feasibility with Infant (1yo)** | **FATAL RISK / PROHIBITED.** Carrying an infant over slippery boulders with no safety ropes is unsafe. | **FEASIBLE IF CONDITIONS CALM.** Infant sits securely in mother's lap wearing life jacket. |
| **Feasibility for 6yo Child** | **HIGH RISK.** Loose gravel causes slipping; steep climbs cause dehydration and exhaustion. | **THRILLING & FUN.** 6-year-old enjoys boat ride, sea spray, and spotting dolphins. |
| **Stroller Capability** | **0% (IMPOSSIBLE).** Stroller would have to be abandoned or carried overhead. | **N/A.** Stroller remains safely in the Innova Crysta at Om Beach parking. |
| **Time Investment** | 3.5 to 4.5 hours of strenuous hiking round-trip in full sun. | 25 to 35 minutes total round-trip on calm water. |
| **Cost** | Free (at the expense of extreme physical exhaustion). | ~₹350–₹500 per seat (or ₹1,500–₹2,000 for private boat hire). |

## Pleasant Window
- **Morning Window (08:00–09:30 AM):** Flat calm waters, gentle sea breeze, no swell. Optimal window for boarding the scenic offshore boat cruise from Om Beach.

---

## Kids / Baby Logistics
- **Stroller Alert:** Strollers are 100% prohibited on the boat and impossible on the cliff trails. Leave in car trunk.
- **Life Jackets:** Mandatory snug-fitting child life jacket for the 6-year-old and infant held securely in parent's lap wearing life vest.
- **Offshore Only:** Keep the cruise offshore to avoid hazardous rock landings with babies.
