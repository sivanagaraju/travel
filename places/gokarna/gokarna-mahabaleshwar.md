# Gokarna Mahabaleshwar Temple (Atmalinga)

**Circuit role:** Must-cover **Gokarna town temple**. Birthday week.  
**When:** **A Sun 15 17:00–18:15** after Kudle/pool **or** skip if the infant is done and do **Mon 16 06:00–08:00**. Same idea on C/F. **E Fri 13 evening** before the birthday.

## Pleasant

A **pleasant** visit is **30–45 minutes** in the **evening window 17:00–20:00** or **early 06:00–08:00**. Morning window typically **06:00–12:30**, then a long close until 17:00. Free. Atmalinga: you see the **top** through a hole.

## Peace

Town temple is busy on Sunday. **Peace** is **Monday 06:00**. If Sunday 15 is the birthday, protect cake time: if the 6-year-old is crashing, **skip Sunday temple** and go Monday. The must is **overnight + dinner in Gokarna**, not this sanctum at all costs.

## Handle kids

- **Dress code is strict:** men **dhoti**, often **bare chest** in the inner sanctum; women **saree or long salwar**. **No beachwear.** Rent a dhoti in town. Pack one.
- Infant in carrier. 6-year-old full-length clothes.
- Footwear off. Stone is hot at 17:00 — socks in the bag for the child if needed.
- Sunday evening **3–4**. Don’t stack it after a 180 km day (**D**): do **Monday 06:00** on D.

## What this place covers

Atmalinga darshan · courtyard · optional annadana if times match · **not** a 3-hour ritual list.
