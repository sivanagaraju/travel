## Conflicts resolved in the itinerary

1. **Champa Shashti 2026:** Prompt said 27 Nov–12 Dec. kukkedevasthanam `/festivals/` HTML is internally messy (Nov 20 / Nov 23 vs a later “November 30 –” fragment). **`/best-time-to-visit/` + Prokerala = 15 Dec 2026, mahotsavam ~1–15 Dec.** Itinerary uses that and still keeps Kukke on a weekday.
2. **Kartika Purnima:** One Murudeshwar aggregator said 15 Nov 2026. Prokerala / multiple panchang pages: **24 Nov 2026**. 15 Nov is Sashti / Soora Samharam.
3. **Eco Beach hours/tickets:** 08:00–18:00 ₹10/₹5 vs 08:30–18:30 ₹20 vs “no fee / 6 AM–7 PM.” Itinerary tells the family to **pay at the gate** and **leave by 18:00**.
4. **Dharmasthala afternoon close ~14:30:** Forces Friday to hit Dharmasthala **before 14:20**, then Karkala **16:00**.
5. **Gokarna→Hubli 3–4 h:** Not shrunk. Flagged.
6. **“1500 km loop”:** Replaced with measured ~550–700 km hired-car total.
6b. **Saturday NH66 order (fixed after skeptic):** First draft did Maravanthe → Idagunji → Eco Beach → *south* to Murudeshwar → Gokarna. That is a ~50 km Honnavar↔Murudeshwar backtrack and implied ~70 km in 15 minutes. **Northbound NH66 is Udupi → Maravanthe → Murudeshwar → Idagunji/Honnavar/Kasarkod → Gokarna.** Recast the 14 Nov clock to that linear sequence; Murudeshwar 15:00–16:00 (after sanctum reopens); Eco Beach 16:55–17:40 (out by 18:00). Legs: Rome2Rio Maravanthe–Murudeshwar 54.5 km / 41 min; distancebetween2 Murudeshwar–Idagunji 20.5 km / 20 min; murudeshwar.in Honnavar 24–27 km; Honnavar–Gokarna ~51 km.
7. **Four bases vs 15 Nov rest vs 12–18 window:** Cannot have Kukke overnight + Udupi 2 n + Gokarna rest on the 15th. Primary: Mangalore 1 + Udupi 1 + Gokarna 4. Documented.
8. **SwaSwara:** Beautiful Om Beach resort — **no young children**. Not used.
9. **Paradise Isle crib:** Agoda vs Expedia conflict → pack a travel cot.
10. **Dandeli safari age:** Sources disagree (all ages / 5+ / 8+ for other activities). **Rejected anyway.**
11. **Namaste Samudra is not Om Beach.** Official Namaste Gokarna: Samudra = **Gokarna Main Beach**, 220 m field path, **no road, no dedicated parking**. Om property is **Namaste Cafe**. Do not send an Innova + infant there. Default Gokarna stay stays **Kudle Beach View** (3-star, pool, sit-out).
12. **Cost snapshot 5 Sep 2026:** Approach A family **~₹1.2 lakh tight / ~₹2.0 lakh comfortable**. Stay rule: **1-night = 3-star**; **Udupi/Gokarna = beach-side**. RNS Residency some OTAs **18+** — verify in writing or use Naveen Beach for Approach F.
