### Airline Infant Baggage, Stroller & Health Policies

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                     FAMILY LUGGAGE & STROLLER ENTITLEMENT                   |
|                                                                             |
|  [ 3 Ticketed Seats ]          [ Infant on Lap ]        [ Gate-Check Pram ] |
|  - Check-in: 15 kg x 3 = 45 kg - Cabin Diaper Bag: 7 kg - 1 Collapsible     |
|  - Cabin: 7 kg x 3 = 21 kg     - Check-in: 0 kg           Stroller: FREE    |
|  - TOTAL CHECKED: 45 KG        - Seated on adult lap    - Door-to-door delivery|
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

1. **Complimentary Stroller Gate-Check (Both IndiGo & Fly91):**
   - Domestic airlines permit **one collapsible stroller or pram per infant completely free of charge**. It does **not** count against your 45 kg checked baggage allowance.
   - **How to execute:** At the check-in desk at HYD, request a bright pink/yellow **"Delivery at Aircraft Door"** tag. Wheel your baby all the way through security, through the departure lounge, and down to the aircraft door. Hand the folded stroller to the ramp crew at the aircraft steps. Upon landing at IXE / HYD, ground staff retrieve the stroller from the forward cargo hold and present it right outside the aircraft door.
2. **Infant Hand Luggage (7 kg Free):**
   - The infant is entitled to one complimentary **7 kg cabin bag**. Dedicate this bag exclusively as the baby care emergency kit:
     - 8 diapers + 2 packs of wet wipes.
     - 2 complete changes of clothes for the infant + 1 clean shirt for each parent (in case of spit-ups).
     - 2 sterile feeding bottles + infant formula powder dispenser.
     - Thermos flask with pre-boiled warm drinking water (exempt from 100 ml security rule for infants).
     - Infant paracetamol drops (Calpol), teething gel, pacifiers, and a muslin swaddle.
3. **Mandatory Documentation:**
   - Carry the original **Municipal Birth Certificate** or Passport for the infant. CISF at airport entry and airline counter agents verify age to confirm lap entitlement.
4. **ATR-72 Turboprop Ear Pressure Safeguards:**
   - Both HYD–IXE (IndiGo) and HBX–HYD (Fly91) routes utilize ATR-72 turboprop aircraft. Cabin pressurization cycles are slightly steeper than wide-body aircraft.
   - **The Golden Rule:** Never allow the baby to sleep during the descent. Wake the infant gently 25 minutes prior to landing and begin bottle feeding, nursing, or giving a pacifier. The swallowing action automatically opens the Eustachian tubes, equalizing middle ear pressure and completely eliminating pain-induced crying.
