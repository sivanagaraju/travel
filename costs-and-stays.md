# Cost, Stays & Flights Master Guide
## Family of 4 (2 Adults + 6-Year-Old + 1-Year-Old Infant) · Nov 2026

Figures are calculated for the **entire family of 4** (2 working IT adults, one 6-year-old child occupying a full seat, and one infant boy turning 1 on **15 Nov 2026**). November is the start of the peak holiday season in Coastal Karnataka—booking flights and stays 45–60 days in advance saves **20–35%**.

## 1. Flights Analysis: Non-Stop Direct Schedules, Timings & Costs

- **2 Adults**: Full seat fare.
- **1 Child (6 years)**: Full adult fare (Indian aviation regulations charge 100% of adult base fare for children ≥2 years).
- **1 Infant (turning 1 on 15 Nov)**: Flat domestic infant lap fee (no separate seat; sits on parent's lap).

### Airline Infant Baggage, Stroller & Health Policies

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                     FAMILY LUGGAGE & STROLLER ENTITLEMENT                   |
|                                                                             |
|  [ 3 Ticketed Seats ]          [ Infant on Lap ]        [ Gate-Check Pram ] |
|  - Check-in: 15 kg x 3 = 45 kg - Cabin Diaper Bag: 7 kg - 1 Collapsible     |
|  - Cabin: 7 kg x 3 = 21 kg     - Check-in: 0 kg           Stroller: FREE    |
|  - TOTAL CHECKED: 45 KG        - Seated on adult lap    - Door-to-door delivery|
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

1. **Complimentary Stroller Gate-Check (IndiGo & Air India Express):**
   - Domestic airlines permit **one collapsible stroller or pram per infant completely free of charge**. It does **not** count against your 45 kg checked baggage allowance.
   - **How to execute:** At the check-in desk at HYD and GOI, request a bright pink/yellow **"Delivery at Aircraft Door"** tag. Wheel your baby through security, through the departure lounge, and through the aerobridge right to the aircraft door. Hand the folded stroller to the ramp crew at the aircraft entrance. Upon landing, ground staff retrieve the stroller from the forward hold and present it right outside the aircraft door.
2. **Infant Hand Luggage (7 kg Free):**
   - The infant is entitled to one complimentary **7 kg cabin bag**. Dedicate this bag exclusively as the baby care emergency kit:
     - 8 diapers + 2 packs of wet wipes.
     - 2 complete changes of clothes for the infant + 1 clean shirt for each parent (in case of spit-ups).
     - 2 sterile feeding bottles + infant formula powder dispenser.
     - Thermos flask with pre-boiled warm drinking water (exempt from 100 ml security rule for infants).
     - Infant paracetamol drops (Calpol), teething gel, pacifiers, and a muslin swaddle.
3. **Mandatory Documentation:**
   - Carry the original **Municipal Birth Certificate** or Passport for the infant. CISF at airport entry and airline counter agents verify age to confirm lap entitlement.
4. **Jet Aircraft Comfort (A320/B737) vs Turboprop (ATR-72):**
   - Returning via South Goa (GOI) utilizes mainline commercial jets (Airbus A320 / Boeing 737) rather than regional turboprops. Jets offer smoother high-altitude cruising, significantly lower cabin noise, spacious overhead luggage bins for diaper bags, and boarding via covered aerobridges rather than steep open-air tarmac stairs.

### Key Budget Insights
- **Cost Reduction from South Goa Exit:** Flying out of South Goa (GOI) instead of Hubli (HBX) saves **~₹3,000 to ₹7,000** on flight tickets alone, completely absorbing the small ₹600–₹800 Goa interstate commercial taxi permit!
- **Savings from Udupi Center Base:** Staying in Udupi town center near Sri Krishna Temple (Samanvay Boutique Hotel) costs ~₹4,500–₹6,000/night compared to ₹6,500–₹9,000/night at Malpe beachfront resorts, saving another **₹4,000–₹6,000** while placing the family within a 2-minute stroll of the temple and iconic satvik dining.
- **Model H-4N vs H-5N:** Model H-4N requires only 2 working days leave (Thu & Fri) and returns Monday night via South Goa. Model H-5N adds a full leisure recovery day on Monday in Gokarna and returns Tuesday night.
- **2-Base Booking Simplicity:** Requires only TWO hotel bookings for the entire trip: **Samanvay Boutique Hotel (Udupi Center, 2 Nights)** + **Kudle Beach View Resort & Spa (Gokarna, 2 Nights)**.
