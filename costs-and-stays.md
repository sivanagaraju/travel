# Cost and stays
## Family of 4 · Nov 2026 · snapshot 5 Sep 2026 · recheck before you pay

Figures are **for the whole family**. November is peak — add **15–25%** if you book late.

**Stay rule**

| Sleep | Book |
|---|---|
| **1 night** (Mangalore, Kukke, Dharmasthala, Murudeshwar) | Clean **3-star** (or temple guest house): restaurant, hot water, cot |
| **Udupi** (1 or 2 nights) | **Malpe beach-side** 3-star — not Car Street |
| **Gokarna** (2–4 nights) | **Kudle or Om beach-side** 3-star, **pool + sit-out** for cake |
| Airbnb | Only **2+ nights**, hosted, crib + Innova parking. Not 1-night city listings |

**Do not:** SwaSwara (no children under 10), hostels, Namaste Samudra (Main Beach, no car gate). Pack a travel cot.
