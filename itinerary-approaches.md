# Six ways to run this family trip — pick one, don’t mix clocks

This is **not** one itinerary with a footnote. It is **six different trip shapes** for the same Hyderabad family of 4 (6-year-old + infant turning 1 on **Sunday 15 Nov 2026**). Each approach covers the **same must-stop list** unless a row in the comparison table says it drops something.

Read this file first if you want to **choose**. Then open the matching day-plan:

| If you choose | Open |
|---|---|
| **A** (recommended default) | [coastal-karnataka-family-itinerary.md](coastal-karnataka-family-itinerary.md) 7-day primary |
| **B** | Same file, 6-day tight section |
| **C–F** | Day tables **in this file** (they are complete clocks, not sketches) |

Stops grouped by town: [places/](places/README.md).  
Cost + one hotel pick per town: [costs-and-stays.md](costs-and-stays.md) (**~₹1.2–2.0 lakh** on A; **3-star** for 1-nights; **beach-side** Udupi/Gokarna).

Hard facts that do **not** change by approach:

- Fly. Do not road/train/bus Hyderabad → coast with an infant.
- Innova Crysta with driver. Infant in the **carrier** or on a lap. Break every 2 hours. No ghat after 18:00.
- NH66 northbound is **Udupi → Maravanthe → Murudeshwar → Idagunji → Eco Beach Kasarkod → Gokarna**. Never Honnavar then *south* to the statue.
- Kukke **not on Sat/Sun**. Champa Shashti 2026 is **15 Dec**, not 27 Nov–12 Dec.
- **15 Nov overnight + birthday dinner = Gokarna.** Whether the 15th is a *rest* day depends on the approach.
- Dandeli **4-hour open jeep safari is out**.

## How to choose (one screen)

| Your #1 priority | Take | You accept |
|---|---|---|
| Infant’s birthday is a **full rest day** (no highway) **and** you will not add 11 Nov | **A** | Friday 13 Nov is a FLAG 260 km temple day; Udupi only 1 night; no Kukke hotel |
| Same as A but fly home **17 Nov** | **B** | No buffer Tuesday; no Yana; packing the morning after one Gokarna catch-up day |
| Want a **Kukke/Dharmasthala hotel night** *and* a rest-day birthday | **C1** | Add **Wed 11 Nov**; trip becomes 8 days / 7 nights |
| Want **Udupi 2 nights** (real Malpe/Kapu pace) *and* a rest-day birthday | **C2** | Add **Wed 11 Nov**; Kukke is a Thursday day-stop, not an overnight |
| Want the original **four hotel bases** on 12–18 Nov | **D** | **Sunday 15 Nov is a transfer day** (cake still in Gokarna at night) |
| Maximum **Gokarna peace first** (infant settles before temples) | **E** | Fly **HYD→HBX** first; Kukke ghat is **last**, on a tired family; Tannirbhavi is a morning not an evening |
| Don’t want a 180 km Saturday; birthday afternoon still in Gokarna | **F** | Sleep **Murudeshwar Sat 14**; Sunday 15 is a **short** north hop (~51–80 km), not a zero-drive rest day |

**Best overall for this family if dates stay 12–18 Nov: Approach A.**  
**Best if you can take 11 Nov off: Approach C2** (kinder Udupi beaches) **or C1** (kinder Kukke).  
**Do not pick D** unless you are willing to celebrate the birthday after a car day.

## Must-cover checklist (every approach except where noted)

Mangalore Tannirbhavi **or** Panambur · Kukke Subrahmanya · Dharmasthala · Chaturmukha Basadi Karkala · Udupi Sri Krishna Matha · Malpe · Kapu Lighthouse Beach · Maravanthe · Idagunji · Eco Beach Kasarkod · Murudeshwar temple & beach · Gokarna Om · Kudle · Mahabaleshwar Temple · birthday dinner in Gokarna · Hubli exit.

Optional, never required: Yana Caves. Dandeli only as coracle/birding on an extra trip — **not** jeep safari.

# Approach A — Birthday-rest clockwise (7 days / 6 nights, 12–18 Nov)
## Recommended default

**Air:** HYD→IXE morning 12 Nov · HBX→HYD evening 18 Nov (prefer Fly91 ~21:25).  
**Idea:** Be **in Gokarna on the night of 14 Nov** so **Sunday 15 Nov is zero highway**. Kukke on **Friday 13** (weekday). One ghat, morning.

| Date | Weekday | Drive (honest) | Night | What |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 Nov | Thu | IXE–beach ~25–45 km, <1.5 h | **Mangalore** | Tannirbhavi or Panambur **evening** |
| 13 Nov | Fri | **FLAG ~260 km, 5–6.5 h driving** | **Udupi/Malpe** | Kukke AM (weekday) → Dharmasthala before 14:20 close → Karkala Basadi 16:00–16:40 → Udupi |
| 14 Nov | Sat | **FLAG ~180 km, ~3 h + stops** | **Gokarna** | Malpe 07:00–09:30, Kapu, Krishna; NH66 **north only**: Maravanthe → Murudeshwar 15:00–16:00 → Idagunji → Eco Beach out by 18:00 → Gokarna |
| **15 Nov** | **Sun** | **<20 km local** | **Gokarna** | **Birthday rest.** Kudle, pool, Om, Mahabaleshwar 17:00, cake dinner |
| 16 Nov | Mon | <30 km | Gokarna | Catch-up beach/temple |
| 17 Nov | Tue | 0–20 km, or Yana FLAG ~96 km round trip | Gokarna | Buffer |
| 18 Nov | Wed | **FLAG 147–168 km, 3–4 h** | Home | Gokarna → HBX |

**Pleasant / peace:** Four Gokarna nights. The infant’s hardest car day is Friday, not his birthday.  
**Handle kids:** Naps in the carrier or on a lap on Fri/Sat. Stroller at Malpe and Eco Beach. Carrier at Kukke, Basadi, Mahabaleshwar.  
**You give up:** Kukke hotel; Udupi 2nd night; the original “four bases” on 12–18.

Full hour-by-hour: [coastal-karnataka-family-itinerary.md](coastal-karnataka-family-itinerary.md).

# Approach C — Add Wednesday 11 Nov (8 days / 7 nights, 11–18 Nov)

This is how you buy **either** a temple overnight **or** a real Udupi second night **without** turning 15 Nov into a highway day. Pick **C1 or C2**, not both.

Kukke on **Thursday 12 Nov** is still a **weekday**.

## C1 — Temple overnight + birthday rest

| Date | Weekday | Drive | Night | What |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 11 Nov | Wed | IXE–beach <1.5 h | **Mangalore** | Tannirbhavi/Panambur evening |
| 12 Nov | **Thu** | **105–110 km, 2.5–3.5 h ghat** (under 180 km; at/over 2.5 h — flag the time) | **Kukke** (<100 m from temple if the guest house is on Ratha Beedi) | Morning flight recovery, leave Mangalore after first nap ~10:30, Kukke darshan **afternoon 15:30–20:00** (avoid 13:30–15:30 close). **No ghat after 18:00** — leave Mangalore by 14:00 at latest; **better leave 10:30** and eat at Kukke |
| 13 Nov | Fri | Kukke→DS 53 km + DS→Karkala 64 km + Karkala→Udupi 38 km ≈ **155 km, ~3–3.5 h** FLAG vs 2.5 h | **Udupi/Malpe** | Dharmasthala **06:30–11:00** + Annadana; Karkala **10:00–14:00** (better than the 16:00 slot); Udupi Krishna evening |
| 14 Nov | Sat | ~180 km FLAG | **Gokarna** | Same linear NH66 as A |
| **15 Nov** | **Sun** | Local | **Gokarna** | **Birthday rest** |
| 16–17 | Mon–Tue | Local | Gokarna | Same as A |
| 18 Nov | Wed | FLAG Hubli | Home | Same as A |

**Pleasant / peace:** Kukke at dusk is quieter than a Friday smash-and-go. Infant sleeps near the temple, not after 260 km.  
**Handle kids:** Guest houses near Kukke rarely have high chairs — pack a mat. Hot water: confirm.  
**You give up:** One extra leave day from work; Udupi still 1 night.

|---|---|---|---|---|

**Handle kids:** Best Malpe window of any 12–18 variant (Friday, not weekend).  
**You give up:** Kukke hotel; one extra work day.

**If you can take 11 Nov, C2 is the gentlest “must-cover everything” trip.** C1 is better only if Sarpa-area overnight / early Kukke morning darshan matters more than Malpe.

# Approach D — Four bases on 12–18 Nov (birthday is a car day)

This is the shape the original hotel list asked for: Mangalore 1n · Kukke **or** Dharmasthala 1n · Udupi 1n · Gokarna 2–3n. It **cannot** also make 15 Nov a rest day.

| Date | Weekday | Drive | Night | What |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 Nov | Thu | <1.5 h | **Mangalore** | Beach evening |
| 13 Nov | Fri | 105–110 km ghat + 53 km ≈ **158 km, ~3.5–4.5 h** FLAG vs 2.5 h | **Dharmasthala** (closer to the next day’s Karkala than Kukke is) | Kukke **09:30–12:30 weekday**, lunch, DS **evening 17:00–20:00**. Arrive DS **before 18:00** (last hour is not a ghat like Kukke, but do not push past 18:00 in the hills) |
| 14 Nov | Sat | ~100 km + Udupi local | **Udupi** | DS Annadana if missed, Karkala **10:00–14:00**, Krishna, **Malpe 16:00 — Saturday evening crowd 4–5, keep it short**. Kapu lighthouse **16:00–18:00** actually works this approach |
| **15 Nov** | **Sun** | **FLAG ~180 km, ~3 h** | **Gokarna** | Leave Udupi **after first nap ~10:30** (skip Sunday Malpe). Linear NH66: Maravanthe photo → Murudeshwar **15:00–16:00** → Idagunji → Eco Beach **out by 18:00** → Gokarna **~19:00**. **Cake 20:00**, not 18:30. Overnight Gokarna **yes**. Rest day **no** |
| 16 Nov | Mon | Local | Gokarna | Real rest / Kudle / Om / Mahabaleshwar 06:00 |
| 17 Nov | Tue | Local | Gokarna | Buffer |
| 18 Nov | Wed | FLAG Hubli | Home | Evening flight |

**Pleasant / peace:** 16 Nov becomes the gentle day. 15 Nov is not.  
**Handle kids:** Birthday cake **after** a car day. Pre-cut the cake plan; one adult stays in the car with the sleeping infant at Eco Beach if needed.  
**You give up:** The “15 Nov is not a transfer day” rule. **Only choose D if that rule is negotiable.**

Variant D-Kukke-night: sleep Kukke 13 Nov instead of DS. Then 14 Nov is the 155 km DS+Karkala+Udupi day (same as C1’s Friday). Still a Sunday transfer.

# Approach E — Reverse: Hubli in, Mangalore out (Gokarna first)

**Air:** HYD→**HBX** 12 Nov (IndiGo ~morning if it exists that day; else previous evening + Hubli hotel — extra night). Return **IXE→HYD** 18 Nov.  
**Car:** Innova **HBX pickup → IXE drop**. Infant in the carrier or on a lap.  
**Idea:** The infant meets the **sea before the ghat**. Birthday rest is natural because you are already in Gokarna.

Southbound NH66 (the reverse of Saturday in A) is **linear south**: Gokarna → Eco Beach / Idagunji / Honnavar → **Murudeshwar** → Maravanthe → Udupi. Still **no backtrack**.

| Date | Weekday | Drive | Night | What |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 Nov | Thu | **FLAG 147–168 km, 3–4 h** Hubli→Gokarna | **Gokarna** | Early HBX arrival or you cannot reach Kudle before 18:00. If the only HBX flight is late, **do not pick E** |
| 13 Nov | Fri | Local | Gokarna | Kudle/Om. Mahabaleshwar 17:00 |
| 14 Nov | Sat | Local | Gokarna | Quiet Saturday on Kudle (Om will be busier) |
| **15 Nov** | **Sun** | Local | **Gokarna** | **Birthday rest** |
| 16 Nov | Mon | ~180 km FLAG south | **Udupi** | Leave ~10:30. Eco Beach (open 08:00, do it **11:00–12:00** before heat), Idagunji 12:00–12:30 (morning window till 13:00), Murudeshwar **15:00–16:00**, Maravanthe photo, Udupi night |
| 17 Nov | Tue | **FLAG ~260 km** Udupi→Karkala→DS→Kukke→Mangalore | **Mangalore** | Karkala **10:00–12:00**, DS Annadana, Kukke **15:30–18:00 weekday**, then **ghat to Mangalore — leave Kukke by 16:00** to avoid ghat after 18:00 (Kukke→Mangalore 2.5–3.5 h). This is the **riskiest ghat** of any approach |
| 18 Nov | Wed | IXE <1 h | Home | Tannirbhavi/Panambur **morning 07:00–09:00** (not evening). Fly IXE→HYD |

**Pleasant / peace:** Best first four days of any option.  
**Handle kids:** Confirm a **morning** HYD→HBX before you lock E. Tuesday 17 is harder than A’s Friday because the family is tired and the ghat is last.  
**You give up:** Mangalore *evening* beach (you get morning instead). Kapu may need to be a 17 Nov dawn add-on or a 16 Nov late arrival skip — **Kapu is south of Udupi**; do it **17 Nov 07:00–08:00** before Karkala, or you drop it. **Do not drop Kapu silently** — if you pick E, **Kapu is 17 Nov 07:00** (17 km, then Karkala inland).

E with Kapu: 17 Nov 07:00 Kapu (no lighthouse climb) → Karkala 10:00 → DS → Kukke 15:30 → Mangalore by 18:00 is **too tight**. Honest E either **starts 11 Nov in Gokarna** or **accepts a very long Tuesday**. If Kapu is mandatory and you want reverse, **add 11 Nov Gokarna** and move the temple belt to two days (16 Udupi+Kapu+Malpe, 17 Karkala–Kukke–Mangalore).

**Recommendation:** Only pick E if a morning HBX flight is real **and** you add 11 Nov **or** you accept Kapu as a 07:00 dash.

# Approach F — Murudeshwar overnight (short Sunday, cake still in Gokarna)

**Air / car:** Same as A (IXE in, HBX out).  
**Idea:** Don’t dump Udupi→Gokarna (~180 km) onto one Saturday. Sleep **at Murudeshwar Saturday night**. Sunday 15 is **Murudeshwar/Honnavar → Gokarna only (~51–80 km, ~1.5 h + two short stops)** plus birthday afternoon.

| Date | Weekday | Drive | Night | What |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 Nov | Thu | <1.5 h | **Mangalore** | Beach evening |
| 13 Nov | Fri | FLAG ~260 km | **Udupi** | Same temple belt as A |
| 14 Nov | Sat | Udupi local + **~102 km** to Murudeshwar ≈ **under 180 km, ~2–2.5 h** intercity after beaches | **Murudeshwar** | Malpe 07:00–09:30, Kapu, Krishna, Maravanthe, **Murudeshwar 15:00–sunset** (statue lighting). Hotel with restaurant + cot (Naveen Beach / RNS / similar — **confirm cot**). **Not** the chaotic main-beach shacks as the infant’s room |
| **15 Nov** | **Sun** | **~51–80 km, ~1.5–2 h** + stops. Under 180 km. **At the 2.5 h line if you linger** | **Gokarna** | Idagunji **08:00–09:00** (morning window), Eco Beach **09:15–10:15** (park empty, pleasant), Gokarna **by 12:00–12:30**. Nap. **Birthday afternoon + dinner**. Not a zero-drive day; **not** a 180 km day |
| 16–17 | Mon–Tue | Local | Gokarna | Full beach days |
| 18 Nov | Wed | FLAG Hubli | Home | Evening flight |

**Pleasant / peace:** Sunday morning Eco Beach is the calmest that park will be all week. Birthday afternoon is protected.  
**Handle kids:** One extra hotel change (harder with a cot/stroller). Murudeshwar Saturday night can be **noisy** near the statue — book **away from the plaza**, still <10 min drive.  
**You give up:** A *fully* static 15 Nov. You **gain** a sane Saturday.

**F is the best 12–18 option if Approach A’s Saturday feels too long but you will not add 11 Nov.**

## Verdict

1. **Dates locked 12–18, birthday must be a rest day → A.** Use **F** if Saturday 14 Nov in A looks impossible when you stare at the infant.  
2. **Can take 11 Nov → C2** (beaches) or **C1** (temple night).  
3. **Four hotel bases more important than a rest-day birthday → D** (say that out loud before booking).  
4. **Gokarna-first fantasy → E only with a morning HBX flight and preferably 11 Nov.**  
5. **Never** mix A’s Saturday clock with D’s Sunday clock in the same booking. One approach, one voucher.
