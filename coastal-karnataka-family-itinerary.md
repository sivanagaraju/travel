# Coastal Karnataka Temple + Beach Circuit

## Family of 4 from Hyderabad — 7-day primary plan (12–18 Nov 2026) + 6-day tight variant

**Who:** 2 working IT professionals, one child ~6 years, one infant boy turning **1 on 15 Nov 2026**. Infant carrier + stroller. Child-friendly pace.
**Must:** Birthday dinner and overnight **15 Nov 2026 (Sunday)** in **Gokarna** at a Kudle/Om-area resort with pool and a private sit-out for cake.
**Airports:** Fly **HYD → IXE** (Mangalore) in; **HBX → HYD** (Hubli) out. Do **not** road/train/bus Hyderabad to the coast with an infant.
**Car:** Private **Innova Crysta with driver**, IXE pickup → HBX drop, **proper baby car seat** (no hammock).
**Research snapshot:** 5 Sep 2026. Fares, flight numbers, and temple hours move — reconfirm before paying.

## How this plan is built

Clockwise **HYD → IXE → Mangalore → Kukke Subrahmanya → Dharmasthala → Karkala (Chaturmukha Basadi) → Udupi → Maravanthe → Murudeshwar → Idagunji → Eco Beach Kasarkod/Honnavar → Gokarna → Hubli HBX → HYD**.

**What the prompt got wrong (corrected from calendars and maps):**

- **Champa Shashti is not 27 Nov–12 Dec 2026.** Independent Kukke guides compiling the 2026 calendar, plus Prokerala, put **Champa Shashti on Tuesday 15 Dec 2026**, with Champashasti Mahotsavam about **1–15 Dec 2026**. 12–18 Nov is **before** that crush. Still keep Kukke off weekends (done). Confirm on [itms.kar.nic.in](https://itms.kar.nic.in) closer to travel.
- **“~1500 km full loop” is not this trip.** Measured coastal IXE→Gokarna via NH66 is ~230–250 km. Kukke/Dharmasthala/Karkala add ~150–180 km. **Gokarna→HBX** adds ~150–168 km. Expect **~550–700 km** of hired-car driving, not 1,500.

- **Do not drive Hyderabad → coast** (~750+ km, ~16 h car / ~18 h bus / ~29 h train).

**Calm vs crowded** on this page: **1 = quiet, 5 = packed.** Ratings are for **this family’s dates and time-of-day**, not a generic guidebook score.

# Beach rules for this family

**Allowed shallow play (marked zone, one adult touching the child):** Malpe morning, Kapu, Kudle, Om cove, Eco Beach park edge, Tannirbhavi/Panambur evening.

**Look-don’t-swim:** Maravanthe, Murudeshwar main statue beach at any busy hour, Gokarna Main on Sunday.

**Skip with a 1-year-old:** St. Mary’s Island boat, Half Moon / Paradise trek, Murudeshwar water sports, banana boat (operators often **8+**), any red-flag monsoon net (you are in **November**, usually yellow/open — still obey the flag).

Malpe “rope in the sea”: treat as **operator safety theatre**, not a swimming pool. Udupi Tourism still tells parents not to let children enter rough water. Your 6-year-old stays where a lifeguard is standing.
