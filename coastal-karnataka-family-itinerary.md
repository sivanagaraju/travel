## St. Mary's Island & Beach Safety Guidelines for Family

1. **St. Mary's Island Infant Protocol:**
   - **NO STROLLERS ON THE ISLAND:** The terrain consists of hexagonal basalt rock columns, coarse shell fragments, and unpaved sand. Strollers cannot roll. **Infant must be carried in an ergonomic front chest carrier (e.g. Ergobaby/Kol Kol)**.
   - **STRICT PLASTIC BAN:** The Udupi district administration strictly confiscates disposable plastic water bottles at Malpe Jetty. Carry drinking water in **stainless steel thermos flasks**.
   - **FOOTWEAR:** Both adults and the 6yo must wear secure, non-slip water shoes or strapped sandals to prevent slipping on wet boat ladders or rocky surfaces.
   - **SUN PROTECTION:** The island has minimal natural shade. Bring wide-brim sunhats, UV sunglasses, and reef-safe baby sunscreen. Complete the visit by 11:30 AM before midday heat peaks.
2. **Beach & Sea Swimming Rules:**
   - **Kudle Beach & Malpe Beach:** Shallow paddling and sand play permitted only in marked safe zones with an adult holding the child's hand.
   - **Maravanthe & Murudeshwar:** Strong undertows and deep drop-offs. Strictly **look-and-photograph only**—no swimming.
